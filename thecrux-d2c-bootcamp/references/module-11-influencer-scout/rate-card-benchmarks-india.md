# India creator rate-card benchmarks

INR ranges Indian D2C brands actually pay in 2026. Treat the listed Modash rate-estimate as a ceiling and the lower end of these ranges as a realistic ask. Most paid deals close 20-40% below the platform estimate, especially when a gifting component is bundled.

## Instagram

| Tier | Followers | Feed post | Reel | Story set (3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | 1k-10k | ₹2,500-7,000 | ₹4,500-12,000 | ₹1,200-3,500 |
| Micro | 10k-100k | ₹15,000-40,000 | ₹25,000-75,000 | ₹7,000-19,000 |
| Mid | 100k-500k | ₹50,000-1,80,000 | ₹90,000-2,40,000 | ₹20,000-48,000 |
| Macro | 500k-1M | ₹1,80,000-4,50,000 | ₹3,20,000-8,00,000 | ₹70,000-1,80,000 |
| Mega | 1M+ | ₹4,50,000-12,00,000 | ₹8,00,000-25,00,000 | ₹1,80,000-5,00,000 |

Reels run 1.4-1.8x feed-post rates. Carousels run 1.1-1.3x. Static feed is the cheapest line.

LinkedIn is undermonetised relative to engagement. The bigger uplift comes from negotiating a topic ownership window (creator agrees not to post for category competitor for 60 days), not from a higher cash rate.

## Multipliers on top of the base rate

Add to the base rate when negotiating the right to use the content beyond the creator's own feed.

| Right | Typical uplift on base rate |
|---|---|
| Organic feed only, no further use | 0% (this is the base) |
| Brand reposts to its own organic social (3 months) | +10-15% |
| Brand uses the creative as paid ad (1 month, India only) | +25-40% |
| Brand uses as paid ad (3 months, India only) | +50-80% |
| Brand uses as paid ad (12 months, worldwide) | +120-200% |
| Brand uses in website / PDP / email permanently | +35-60% |
| Brand whitelists the creator's handle for paid (allowlisting) | +60-100% on top of paid usage |

Almost every Indian creator starts the conversation with no usage rights and bills cash for the post only. The brand's job is to surface usage rights in the first email so it lands in the rate, not as a surprise ask after the contract.

## Affiliate-and-gifting trade-offs

Most Indian creators below 50k followers will accept a hybrid deal: smaller cash + product + affiliate commission. Below 20k followers many will accept gifting + affiliate only. Use this to extend a small budget across more creators.

| Tier | Pure gifting works? | Gifting + affiliate works? | Hybrid (50% cash + gifting + affiliate)? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | Often yes | Yes | Yes (only sweetens) |
| Micro | Rarely | Sometimes | Yes |
| Mid | No | No | Sometimes (if brand is aspirational fit) |
| Macro+ | No | No | Almost never |

Affiliate commissions on Indian D2C deals typically run 10-15% on first-order revenue, sometimes 5-7% on subscription orders if applicable. Higher commissions (20-25%) are reserved for new product launches where the creator is also a beta tester.

## Discounts the brand actually gets

When a brand walks in with a clean ask, fast payment terms (NEFT in 7 days, not 45), and creative freedom that respects the creator's voice, the close rate jumps and the actual paid rate drops. Indian creators typically discount 20-30% off the platform estimate when:

1. The brand asks for one deliverable, not a bundle that bloats production time
2. Payment is committed within 7-14 days of post going live
3. The brand provides a clean brief and does not micromanage the script
4. The brand offers a small affiliate code as a tail
5. The brand commits to one repost on its own organic feed (free reach for the creator)

Stacking 3 of the 5 above usually closes around 30% below the platform estimate. Stacking all 5 closes at the lower end of the table ranges in this document.

## What never moves

A few things almost never get negotiated down. Don't ask:

- Top-tier dermatologists, gynaecologists, qualified nutritionists. Their rate reflects the credibility lift their post gives the brand; that does not bend on volume.
- Creators with under 5% paid-post ratio and 5%+ engagement rate. They are scarce and they know it.
- Creators with a wait-list for collabs (usually mid-tier in fashion, beauty, wellness during festive season Oct-Feb). They negotiate up, not down, during peak.

Walk away from the deal early in these cases or commit to the listed rate. Trying to grind down a creator who has a wait-list burns the relationship before the first post.
