# Influencer negotiation playbook

How to take a shortlisted creator from "this looks good" to "signed and shipped" without overpaying, getting trapped on usage rights, or burning the relationship for the next campaign.

## The five things every deal has to specify

Get these in writing before the brief lands in the creator's inbox. Missing any one is where most Indian D2C-to-creator deals fall apart at the end.

1. **Deliverables:** exact count and format. "One reel" not "social content". Include the runtime (30s vs 90s changes the price). Story sets specify the slide count (3 stories is the default, not 5).
2. **Usage rights:** what the brand can do with the creative beyond the creator's own feed. Default to organic feed only. Anything more sits on top of the base rate (see `rate-card-benchmarks-india.md`).
3. **Exclusivity window:** how long the creator agrees to not post for a category competitor. Default 30 days, ask for 60-90 in tight-competition categories like skincare and femcare.
4. **Approval workflow:** does the brand get to review and request one revision before the post goes live, or is it post-then-flag. Default: one round of revisions on the script or caption, none on the final cut.
5. **Payment terms:** amount, currency, timeline. NEFT 14 days after post goes live is normal. 30 days is the slowest acceptable. Some agencies will push 45-60; refuse unless the creator's reach is unique.

## The deliverable menu

The shortlist of deliverables Indian D2C brands actually buy. Mix and match into a package.

### Instagram

| Deliverable | What it is | When to buy it |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post (static or carousel) | Single image or up to 10 slides | Long-tail SEO on the creator's grid, looks searchable |
| Reel | 15-90s vertical video | Discovery reach; the default ask for awareness campaigns |
| Story set (3 slides) | 3 consecutive 24h stories with link sticker, poll, or CTA | Conversion / click; pair with a discount code |
| Reel + 3-story set | Combined deal | The bread-and-butter package; usually 1.6-1.8x reel-only price |
| IG Live appearance | 30-60min on creator's live | New product launch with Q&A; rare; premium-priced |

### YouTube

| Deliverable | What it is | When to buy it |
|---|---|---|
| Dedicated video | 5-12 min entire video about the product | Big launches; gets long-tail YouTube search |
| Integration (60-90s) | Single sponsor slot inside a video on a different topic | Awareness; cheapest YouTube ask |
| Shorts | 15-60s vertical | Trial of YouTube-as-platform; lower commitment |
| End-card mention | 15-30s outro nod | Cheapest tail-end ask |

### LinkedIn

| Deliverable | What it is | When to buy it |
|---|---|---|
| Thought piece | 200-400 word post tying the brand to a category insight | B2B awareness; founder-to-founder credibility |
| Carousel | 8-12 slide story slide deck | Educational content; high save rate |
| Newsletter mention | Section in the creator's regular newsletter | Recurring touchpoint; great for SaaS |

## Standard contract clauses (the safe defaults)

These go into every outreach email or contract draft as the starting position. The creator can negotiate up; the brand should rarely concede past these.

```
- Posting window: 7 days from agreed go-live date (creator schedules within window)
- Caption / script: brand provides 3 key messages and required disclosure; creator writes the actual copy
- Revisions: 1 round on script or caption before final production; no revisions after final cut shared
- Disclosure: "#ad" or "Paid partnership with @BRAND" tag, India ASCI compliant
- Exclusivity: 30 days from go-live, no posts for direct category competitor
- Usage rights: organic feed only; brand may not paid-amplify, repost or repurpose
- Payment: NEFT 14 days after post goes live, full amount
- Removal: creator agrees not to delete the post for 12 months without notice
```

If the deal expands beyond the default, add ONLY what's needed:

```
Add for paid amplification:
- Brand may run the creative as paid social ad for [DURATION] in [GEO]
- Rate uplift: see usage rights table

Add for tighter exclusivity:
- Exclusivity extended to 60 / 90 days from go-live; creator confirms no other category sponsor was booked in this window before signing

Add for repost rights:
- Brand may repost the creative to its own organic social channels for 90 days
- Rate uplift: 10-15%

Add for whitelisting (allowlisting):
- Creator grants the brand temporary advertiser access to their handle to run paid ads as the creator
- Duration, rate uplift, exit terms specified
```

## Counter-offer scripts

When the platform-estimated rate is higher than the brand's budget, which is most of the time, the brand's job is to lower the rate without lowering perceived value. Three scripts that work.

### Script 1: Anchor with the platform estimate, then trade

```
Hi [CREATOR],

We love [SPECIFIC POST OR REEL], the way you [SPECIFIC THING] is exactly
the tone we want around our [PRODUCT].

Our plan is to bring [PRODUCT] to your audience as a [REEL / FEED + STORY SET].
Modash flagged your rate at ₹[X]. We can commit ₹[X * 0.7] for the [PACKAGE]
with payment NEFT in 7 days and one round of script review only.

Bonus we can stack:
- One repost on our own Instagram (extra ~80k reach)
- 15% affiliate code on first-order revenue, your audience uses your name
- Product seeded to you regardless of how this conversation ends

Let me know if this works. Happy to talk on a quick call if helpful.

[FOUNDER]
[BRAND]
```

Stack 3 of: fast payment, one revision cap, repost on brand channel, affiliate, gifting. Indian creators usually close at 65-75% of platform estimate when 3+ are on the table.

### Script 2: Hybrid (cash + affiliate)

For nano and micro creators when the cash budget is tight.

```
Hi [CREATOR],

Want to do a hybrid deal: ₹[Y] cash + product seeded + 15% affiliate on
[DURATION] with your unique discount code. One reel + 3-story set,
ASCI compliant.

For most creators in your tier, the affiliate ends up being 1.5-2x the cash
component if the audience converts. We track via [SHOPIFY / WEBENGAGE /
ATTRIBUTION TOOL] and pay monthly NEFT.

Open to it?
```

### Script 3: Premium creator, no negotiation

For dermatologists, gynaecologists, top-tier voices. The brand pays the listed rate and asks for credibility-lifting clauses instead of a discount.

```
Hi Dr [NAME],

Your platform rate is ₹[X] for a reel. We're comfortable at ₹[X], and we'd
like to negotiate two things in return:

1. We license the creative for our own organic social for 90 days (no paid)
2. Six-month category exclusivity in [SUB-CATEGORY], we know this is long
   but it would be load-bearing for our launch

Happy with the rate, just want clarity on these two upfront.

[FOUNDER]
[BRAND]
```

## When to walk away

A deal that looks borderline is usually worse than no deal. Walk if:

- Creator wants the rate in advance, full amount, before posting. Standard is 50% advance for first-time deals only with a clear posting window.
- Creator refuses any usage clause or revision pass. Indicates a difficult production process.
- `paid_post_ratio_30d` over 50%. The audience is sponsorship-fatigued.
- `fake_follower_pct` over 25%. The reach math will not work regardless of rate.
- Creator has run paid for a direct competitor inside the exclusivity window the brand needs.
- Audience location does not match the brand's shipping geography (Indian brand, audience 40% non-India = the conversion math breaks).

## After the post goes live

The campaign doesn't end at "posted". Three things to do in the 14 days after:

1. **Pull post metrics on day 1, day 3, day 7, day 14.** Modash will track them automatically if you have a paid seat. If not, ask the creator for a screenshot of insights.
2. **Track click-through or affiliate revenue separately.** Modash's auto-tracking attributes Shopify sales by creator if Shopify is connected. Without that, attribute via a unique code per creator.
3. **Decide whether to renew BEFORE the exclusivity window ends.** If the creator outperformed, lock the next post under the same rate. If they underperformed, let the exclusivity window expire and shop again.

A renewed creator deal closes 20-30% faster than a first-time deal. Build the second-post conversation into the first contract from day one.
