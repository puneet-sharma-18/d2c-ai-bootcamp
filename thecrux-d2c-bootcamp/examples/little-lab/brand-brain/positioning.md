# Positioning, Little Lab

Little Lab is a paediatrician-reviewed D2C baby skincare brand for Tier-1 metro parents (28 to 38, household income ₹15L+, dual income, ingredient-list readers) who stopped trusting the "natural" and "toxin-free" framing the category sells. Every SKU lists the full ingredient list top to bottom on the front of the bottle, names the active and prints the percentage where the regulator allows it, and is reviewed by a paediatrician panel before it ships. Voice is technical, warm and no-nonsense. Customers enter via a specific problem (cradle cap, dry patches, bath time tantrums), trust us with the next SKU because the first one delivered, and stay because the back of the bottle is what closes the loop. The wedge is transparency: we list percentages where Mother Sparsh says "ayurvedic blend" and stops; we publish the lab reports where The Mom's Co says "toxin-free"; we are tighter on skincare than Mylo's app-and-everything play; we will never carry strollers like R for Rabbit; we do not compete with Mamaearth on the words it owns. We never compete on price. We never use "natural", "pure", "gentle", "toxin-free" or "100% safe". CDSCO compliance is non-negotiable. Paediatric panel sign-off is the gate for every claim above "for dry, irritated skin".
