## What is missing

- A creative for the paediatrician-referred persona that names the clinic (consent on file from Dr. Mehta at Hinduja).
- A creative for the 2-in-1 Bath and Hair Wash that pre-empts the "no foam" objection (currently the 3.9-star SKU; education at the ad layer might reduce the bad reviews).
- A Tier-2 creative variant. Currently we under-run Tier-2 because the Tier-1 lookalikes do not transfer well; we have not tested a Tier-2-native creative yet.

## What we will not run (voice rules)

- "Toxin-free", "100% natural", "100% safe" creative. Banned. Competitor framing or unscientific.
- "Gentle baby lotion" without a named ingredient backing it. Banned.
- "Premium" or "luxury" headline. Banned, price-race trap.
- "Elevate", "unlock", "in today's fast-paced world" copy. AI tells, fails the brand sniff test.
- Curative claims ("treats eczema", "cures cradle cap"). CDSCO claim wall.
