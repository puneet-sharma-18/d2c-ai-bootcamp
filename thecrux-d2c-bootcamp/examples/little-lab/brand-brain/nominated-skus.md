# Nominated SKUs for the Workshop

Five SKUs picked for the later sessions (Content Lead, Performance Marketer, Storefront, Marketplace Editor). Pick reflects revenue weight, customer journey position and the moments we want to win for the rest of the year.

| # | SKU | Why nominated |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Newborn Daily Lotion (LL-NDL-200, ₹549) | Largest unit volume across D2C and Amazon. Most reviews. The repeat-purchase anchor; the Cradle Cap cohort moves into this within 60 days. Content Lead and Performance Marketer cycle most of the retention copy through this. |
| 2 | Cradle Cap Balm (LL-CCB-050, ₹449) | Conversion hero (3.1% CR on Shopify, highest in the catalogue). Highest margin SKU at 69%. The only SKU paediatricians recommend by name. First-touch acquisition for the doctor-referred persona, which is the single highest-trust entry point. |
| 3 | 2-in-1 Bath and Hair Wash (LL-BHW-250, ₹599) | The most fragile SKU. 3.9 average rating because customers expect SLS-style foam and we do not deliver foam. The PDP rewrite this session is the single biggest CR lift on the catalogue. Voice rules and proof points get tested hardest here. |
| 4 | Diaper Rash Cream (LL-DRC-030, ₹329) | Marketplace acquisition workhorse on Amazon. Single-SKU pickups dominate, so it underperforms on AOV but over-indexes on new-customer acquisition. Tests whether the marketplace listing can carry the brand voice without the founder content layer. |
| 5 | Cold-pressed Massage Oil (LL-COM-200, ₹599) | Quick-commerce growth SKU (impulse buy, 18% week-on-week growth in April). Lowest margin in daily care at 57%, which means platform commission economics need the closest watch. Performance Marketer tests the "no mineral oil" angle here. |

## Landing page nomination

For the Storefront + Performance Marketer sessions, the landing page we will workshop is:

**https://littlelab.in/products/cradle-cap-balm**

Why this page: it has the highest CR on the catalogue (3.1%), the most paediatrician-referred traffic, and the active ingredient and percentage (piroctone olamine 1% w/w) are currently buried two scrolls down, which is the whole brand differentiator. Reworking this PDP fixes the largest gap between brand voice and customer experience.
