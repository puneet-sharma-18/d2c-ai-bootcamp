# voice-dna/

Five to ten samples of writing that *sounds like the brand*. PDP body copy, post-purchase emails, social posts, packaging inserts, founder LinkedIn / Instagram caption, WhatsApp replies. The fewer source files we have to interpret what "Little Lab voice" is, the more later teammates default to generic D2C tone. The point of this folder is to remove that ambiguity.

Every later session reads from here:

- Brand Brain (Session 1) re-extracts "always" and "never" phrases here if the voice test scores under 4.
- Content Lead (Session 4) mimics this tone in the 30-day calendar and pieces.
- Performance Marketer (Session 6) re-uses winning hooks for ad copy.
- Storefront (Session 7) leans on PDP samples here for the rewrites.

## Voice fingerprint, in three sentences

We sound like a second-time founder with a paediatrician partner in the team, talking to a parent who already reads the back of the bottle. We use specific words ("piroctone olamine 1% w/w", "coco-glucoside", "no fragrance, no fillers") and never abstract ones ("natural", "pure", "gentle", "toxin-free"). We assume the reader is smart, will fact-check the percentage, and will close the tab the moment a sentence starts with "In today's fast-paced world".
