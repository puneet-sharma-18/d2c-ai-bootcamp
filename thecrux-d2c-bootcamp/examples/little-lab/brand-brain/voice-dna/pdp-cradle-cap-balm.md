## Long description

If your baby has cradle cap, you have read the back of three balms and not found the word that actually does the work. The word is piroctone olamine. We list it. We list the percentage. We list it on the front, not in a 6-point footnote.

Cradle Cap Balm is paediatrically reviewed for newborns. We chose piroctone olamine at 1% w/w because it has thirty years of safety data on infant skin and because we did not want to dress it up. Apply twice a day on the scalp, leave for fifteen minutes, wash off with the 2-in-1 Bath and Hair Wash. Most parents see flakes lift in four to seven days.
