## Long description

The Newborn Daily Lotion is what we make for the parent who has read the back of three baby lotions and could not pronounce half. We list every ingredient on the front of the bottle, top to bottom, in the order of concentration. The list is short on purpose. The list does not include fragrance, mineral oil, parabens, or anything we cannot defend in front of a paediatrician.

Apply after a bath or a nappy change. A pea-sized amount covers the arm and the chest of a newborn. The lotion absorbs in under thirty seconds. The bottle lasts about six weeks at daily use on a six-month-old.

## What is in it

- Aqua, glycerin (humectant), shea butter, jojoba oil, sunflower oil, allantoin, panthenol (provitamin B5), vitamin E
- Stabilisers and preservatives at the lowest effective dose, named on the back
- No fragrance. No SLS. No mineral oil. No parabens. No PEG. No phenoxyethanol.

## What it does

- Replaces the moisture lost during baths and nappy changes
- Calms minor dry patches in two to three days for most babies
- Layers under the Cradle Cap Balm if your baby has both
