## What carries across these

- We answer fast (under ninety minutes during business hours). Priya handles most. Riya signs the ones where stakes are real (delivery failure, paediatrician account, B2B inquiry).
- We say no, in plain words, when the product does not fit. "We would not recommend it" beats a hedge that ends in a return.
- We admit weakness without spinning. "The Aurangabad hub is the friction point." "The 50ml runs out fast." No "we are committed to ongoing improvement".
- We do not push subscription, upsell or "while you're here". One question, one answer, payment link or honest no.
- We do not match competitor price on hero SKUs. We explain the difference and offer a fourteen-day refund instead.
