## Themes the founder is watching

- "Doctor recommended this by name" / "paediatrician approved the ingredient list" — paediatrician trust shows up unprompted across cities and SKUs. The single highest-trust entry into the brand.
- "Ingredient list" / "back of the bottle" — the brand tagline shows up in customer language. Voice of Customer should cluster these as confirmation that the wedge is landing.
- 2-in-1 Bath and Hair Wash lather complaint is a known PDP gap (three reviews in 18 say it does not lather). Fix is education on the PDP, not formulation change. Storefront Specialist owns the rewrite.
- Tier-2 delivery delay (Nashik, Coimbatore) raised in support tickets and reviews. 3PL switch in April moved Tier-2 to a worse courier. Ops Manager owns the rollback decision.
- Subscription demand stated explicitly by repeat buyers. We do not run a subscription yet. Retention Manager designs the calendar-reminder fallback for Q2 and a real subscription for Q3.
- Cradle Cap Balm at 100ml is the most-requested SKU extension. Currently the 50ml runs out in 2 to 3 weeks at twice-daily application.
- Price objection on Newborn Daily Lotion (₹549) versus Mother Sparsh (₹529). The objection softens after first use; the bundle is the conversion lever, not the price.
