## Top pick, @drmehek.parenting (cre_018)

**Why this creator**
Dr Mehek is the highest-credibility parenting voice in the pool: a practising child psychologist with 95% credibility score, 7.1% engagement rate on 118k followers and a 4.4% organic growth rate. Her content frame ("evidence-based parenting in 60 seconds") maps cleanly to Little Lab's positioning ("for the parent who reads the back of the bottle"). She is the bridge between the paediatrician-trust persona and the ingredient-list-reader persona in CLAUDE.md Section 5.

**The fit on the brand DNA**
- Audience: 88% female, 82% age 25-44, 74% India with US/UAE/GB diaspora tail. Mumbai + Delhi + Bangalore + Hyderabad + Pune comprise 52% of followers, matching Little Lab's Tier-1 metro target.
- Voice: technical, warm, no-nonsense. Mirrors the brand's own three-word voice declaration.
- Price tier: audience indexes high on books, wellness and parenting research, the same audience that pays ₹549 for a lotion when it has a paediatrician's review on the back.
- Engagement health: ER 7.1% on a mid-tier account is exceptional. Audience is listening, not scrolling.

**The five red-flag checks**
1. Credibility: PASS (5% fake-follower share)
2. Audience geo: PASS (74% India + 23% Western diaspora that Little Lab also ships to via D2C)
3. Engagement for tier: PASS (7.1% vs 2-5% mid-tier floor, well above)
4. Saturation: PASS (13% paid-post ratio, audience trusts the recommendation channel)
5. Competitor traps: PASS (Flintobox in recent collabs is adjacent kid product, not skincare; Mother Sparsh and The Moms Co not in 90d list)
