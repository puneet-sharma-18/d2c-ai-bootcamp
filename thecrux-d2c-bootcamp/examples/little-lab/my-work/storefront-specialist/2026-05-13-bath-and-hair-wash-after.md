## Description (350 words)
We need to talk about lather.

If you have used a baby wash before, you know the routine. Squeeze a generous amount onto your palm. Add water. Watch the foam build up. Apply, rinse, repeat. The foam was the proof: more lather, more clean.

The foam was a sodium lauryl sulphate (SLS) doing what SLS does. SLS is the most common foaming agent in shampoos and washes. It is also a known irritant to the developing skin barrier on a newborn's scalp. The paediatric literature is clear on this. The newborn's scalp is still producing its lipid barrier in the first 12 weeks; SLS strips that barrier faster than it can re-form.

So we do not use SLS. We use coco-glucoside and decyl glucoside instead. They clean, but they do not foam.

Here is what that means for you. Your baby's bath looks different. You will not see a head of bubbles. The water in the tub will look like water, not like a bubble bath. The wash will feel slightly slippery between your fingers, like very thin soap. Massage it onto your baby's scalp and skin for about 30 seconds, then rinse. The water will run clean. Your baby will be clean.

The 2-in-1 part is the body and hair on the same bottle. The same surfactant blend works on both. We tested the formula across 60 paediatrician-referred parents over 18 months before launching. The feedback that drove the final formulation: "I trust no-lather once I understand why."

This page is for the parents who are not yet sure. Read it once. Try it for 5 baths. If you are not convinced your baby is getting clean without the foam, we refund.

## FAQ
1. **Why no lather?** SLS is the foaming agent. SLS strips the newborn scalp's natural oil barrier. We left it out, so the foam is gone.
2. **Is it still cleaning if it does not foam?** Yes. Coco-glucoside and decyl glucoside lift dirt and oil without foaming. Rinse and the water runs clear.
3. **Can I use it daily?** Yes, designed for daily use from newborn onwards.
4. **What if my baby has cradle cap?** Use our Cradle Cap Balm at bedtime, this wash for the morning. They are designed to work together.
5. **Will it work on my older toddler too?** The formula works from newborn to ~3 years. For 3+, switch to our Toddler Body Wash.
