# Gap analysis — 2-in-1 Bath and Hair Wash 250ml

**Current page emphasises**: Generic claims ("gentle and mild", "natural ingredients", "no harsh chemicals"). Uses two banned words per CLAUDE.md ("natural", "gentle without proof"). Saves time at bath time as the value prop.

**What customers actually care about (top theme from VoC)**: 2-in-1 Wash lather complaint, 8 of 87 messages (9%). The complaint is "doesn't lather at all, doesn't feel like it's working." Customers expect foam = clean.

**What competitors emphasise that we do not**: Mother Sparsh and The Mom's Co both lead with "tear-free" + "gentle" but then explain via the active list. Mamaearth leads with "mild & gentle for baby's delicate skin" but lathers more (uses SLES). The Mom's Co bath wash specifically explains low-foaming as "gentle on the eyes". We do not explain the no-lather choice at all.

**What the page does not address that 30%+ of buyers ask in support**: "Is the product working if it doesn't foam?" The support tickets (28 in the last 30 days, 6 specifically about this) and the WhatsApp messages all carry the same question. The current PDP does not pre-empt it.

**The single biggest conversion blocker (best guess)**: Mismatch between customer expectation (lather = clean) and product reality (SLS-free, no lather by design). The page must educate, not hide. Hide-and-hope is the current strategy and it shows: 41% cart abandonment, 3.9 rating (the 2-star reviews all mention lather).
