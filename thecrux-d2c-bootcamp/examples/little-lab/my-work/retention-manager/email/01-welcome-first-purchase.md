# Email Template — Welcome / First Purchase Follow-up
Trigger: 7 days after first-ever order, email opened or not
Segment: First-time buyers
Tone: Founder voice, story-led. Email gets the longer story; WhatsApp gets the moment.
Allowed personalisation tokens: {first_name}, {first_sku_name}, {customer_city_if_known}

## Subject line (3 variants)
1. About the bottle you just opened
2. {first_name}, a short note from the founder
3. Why your {first_sku_name} probably looks different

## Body
Hi {first_name},

You bought from us last week. Riya here, founder. I write this email by hand to every first-time customer for the first 90 days of their Little Lab life. Then I stop, so I don't crowd your inbox.

A few things you might notice about the bottle:

The back label has eleven ingredients listed. Each one named. Three of them carry the percentage. This is on purpose. The regulator allows us to print percentages where we have them; we have decided to use that allowance to its full extent. No other Indian baby skincare brand does this consistently, and that is the wedge we are betting the company on.

If you are using the 2-in-1 Bath and Hair Wash, you may have noticed it does not lather. That is also on purpose. The foaming agent (SLS) strips the developing oil barrier on a newborn's scalp. We left it out. Massage for 30 seconds, rinse, the water runs clean. Most parents trust the no-lather feel after the third bath.

If something is not working for you in the first 14 days, hit reply on this email. Not the support inbox. I'll see it personally.

One last thing. We are working on a subscribe-and-save (launching June 10) because eight of you wrote in last month asking for it. If that is useful to you, reply to this email with "subscribe" and I'll add you to the early-access list.

Welcome to Little Lab.

Riya
Founder

P.S. If you want to see who else is on the paediatric review panel, the list is at littlelab.in/panel. Add your paediatrician if she's not on it.

## CTA
Soft. Reply with "subscribe" or any other word; either gets a personal response.

## When NOT to send
- Customer also exists in the support-ticket open list (handle the issue first)
- Customer's order was returned or refunded (different email)
- Customer's email bounced on the order confirmation (skip this welcome, retry on next order)

## Compliance
- Promotional category (mentions subscribe-and-save). Footer: unsubscribe link mandatory.
- DPDP Act / CAN-SPAM compliant: clear sender, clear unsubscribe, no purchased list.
