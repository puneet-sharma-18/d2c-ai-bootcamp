# Email Template — Day-7 Post-Purchase Check (Cradle Cap Balm)
Trigger: 7 days after a Cradle Cap Balm order, no support ticket opened
Segment: Cradle Cap Balm first-time buyer
Tone: Founder voice. Specific to the SKU's expected use timeline.
Allowed personalisation tokens: {first_name}

## Subject line (3 variants)
1. How is the cradle cap timeline going?
2. Day 7 check-in on the balm
3. {first_name}, one question about your balm

## Body
Hi {first_name},

It is Day 7 since you got the Cradle Cap Balm. By now, most parents either see real progress or are wondering if anything is happening at all.

The expected timeline:
- Day 3: flakes loosen
- Day 7: visible clearing on most cases
- Day 10 to 14: full clearing on most cases

If you are not seeing change by Day 14, hit reply. Either the active is not the right fit (cradle cap can be confused with eczema or contact dermatitis, and the actives are different), or we have an application question. Either way, I will personally write back.

If it cleared, send me a quick "cleared" — we keep informal notes for our paediatric panel and your data point helps.

When you are ready, the next SKU most cradle-cap parents move to is the Newborn Daily Lotion. Same ingredient transparency, fragrance free. Linked below if useful.

Riya

## CTA
- "Cleared" reply → log to Retention Manager / VoC and tag for the panel digest
- "Not yet" reply → triage to founder personally within 24 hours
- "Show me the lotion" link → PDP for Newborn Daily Lotion

## When NOT to send
- Customer has already left a review or sent a positive WhatsApp
- Customer has an open ticket (handle that first)
- Customer is paediatrician-referred (different bundle follow-up, see WhatsApp 04)
