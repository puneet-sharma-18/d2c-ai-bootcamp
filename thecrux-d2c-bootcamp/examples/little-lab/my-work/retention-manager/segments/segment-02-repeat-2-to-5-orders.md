# Segment — Repeat Buyers (2 to 5 orders)

## Definition (rule)
Customers with 2 to 5 lifetime orders, AND last order in last 90 days, AND opted in to email AND/OR WhatsApp marketing.

## Size estimate
~290 customers (from Shopify cohort pull on 2026-05-13). Smaller than first-timers but higher LTV per customer.

## What this segment cares about
From VoC repeat-buyer voice:
- The new SKU they want us to launch (face cream for moms is the #1 ask)
- Subscribe-and-save they wish existed
- Referral perks for the friends they recommend in their housing-society WhatsApp groups
- Recognition (the "I'm a regular" feeling)

## Templates that fire for this segment
- `whatsapp/01-replenishment-prompt.md` — 35 days after Cradle Cap Balm; parallel templates needed for Newborn Daily Lotion (45-day window) and other SKUs
- `whatsapp/04-paediatrician-bundle-followup.md` — if CRM tag matches
- Subscribe-and-save early-access email (Jun 02 newsletter, see Content Lead calendar slot 25)
- Referral kit (POWER scope, not in this run)

## How to retarget if needed
- Meta Custom Audience: upload email + WhatsApp opted-in customers. Use for "Social proof" creative (Performance Marketer Angle 2) — repeat buyers are credible advocates.
- LAL (lookalike) audience: build a 1% lookalike on this segment for prospecting. Higher LTV signal than the first-time-buyer LAL.
