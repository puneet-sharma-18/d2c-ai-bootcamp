# WhatsApp Template — 60-Day Win-Back (2+ Order Customer)
Trigger: 60 days since last order AND customer has 2+ historical orders AND opted in to WhatsApp marketing
Segment: Lapsed-but-was-repeat (high-LTV recovery candidate)
Tone: Founder voice, direct, no urgency tricks
Allowed personalisation tokens: {first_name}, {last_sku_short_name}

## Body
Hi {first_name}, Riya here. You bought from us a couple of times and I noticed you have not in a while. No pitch, just a real question — is there something we are doing wrong, or did your kid age out of the {last_sku_short_name}? Either answer helps me.

## CTA
Open reply, no link, no discount code. The point is the dialogue, not the conversion.

## When NOT to send
- Customer has explicitly unsubscribed
- Customer has an open complaint or returns ticket
- Customer's last order was a one-and-done (1 order ever) — different template
- The 60-day window crosses a known seasonal pause (e.g. moved cities, on maternity break)

## Compliance
- Promotional opt-in required (this is marketing, not service)
- Footer: "Reply STOP to unsubscribe"
- Marketing conversation tier on Meta pricing
