## Cohort cuts
**First-time buyer voice**: The trigger is almost always a specific problem (cradle cap, dry patches, bath time crying). The barrier is price at shelf. The closer is the ingredient list + paediatrician name on the listing. Average time-to-first-purchase from discovery: 12 days (from CRM).

**Repeat buyer voice**: Repeat buyers do not talk about ingredients anymore — they have moved past that. They talk about (a) the new SKU they want us to launch (face cream is the #1 ask), (b) the subscribe-and-save they wish existed, (c) referring friends in their housing society WhatsApp groups.

## Discrepancies with CLAUDE.md
- **Persona age band**: CLAUDE.md says 28-38. D2C data confirms. Amazon data says 32-42 is more accurate for the paediatrician-referred segment, which is the second-largest persona (13% of mentions). CLAUDE.md Section 5 should be updated to acknowledge the older Amazon segment.
- **Discovery mix**: CLAUDE.md says 40% Instagram, 25% paediatrician, 20% WhatsApp groups, 15% Amazon search. The data suggests paediatrician-referred is closer to 13 to 15% of mentions but higher LTV. Worth a separate cohort-LTV analysis in Module 9.
- **Channel mix**: CLAUDE.md says 55% D2C. The review-mention ratio (D2C 28 vs Amazon 14 = 67/33) matches roughly. No discrepancy here.
