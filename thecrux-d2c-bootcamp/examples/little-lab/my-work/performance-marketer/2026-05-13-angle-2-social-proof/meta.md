# Meta Ads — Angle 2: Social Proof (Ingredient Transparency)

Anchored on: VoC theme #2 (ingredient transparency is the reason to switch, 16%)
Source: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 2, persona "Mumbai/Bengaluru metro mom 30-36"

---

## Meta Ad 1
- Format: Static
- Hook: "I have been the parent who reads the back of the bottle for 6 years."
- Primary text: "You are the first brand that printed what I was already reading." — {customer-C}, Amazon, Apr 2026. We did not buy this review. We printed 11 ingredients on the back of the bottle and she found us.
- Headline: "Read the back."
- Description: "11 ingredients. Each named."
- CTA: Shop Now
- Image / video brief: Tight crop on the bottle's back label, slight bokeh. The review text overlaid in lower-third in clean serif.
- Source citation: VoC theme 2, {customer-C} verbatim
- Why this works: Verbatim customer quote in customer's voice. The "I have been the parent who reads the back of the bottle for 6 years" line is the brand thesis, written by a customer.

## Meta Ad 2
- Format: Reel (10 seconds)
- Hook: "Three baby lotions. One of them tells you what's in it."
- Primary text: One of these has 11 ingredients listed by name. The other two stop at "ayurvedic blend" and "plant-powered extracts". Read the back. Then decide.
- Headline: "Eleven vs five vs three."
- Description: "The full list is the close."
- CTA: Shop Now
- Image / video brief: Three bottles, ours and two unnamed competitors at the same camera distance. Hand flips each one. Voice-over reads ingredient count from each label.
- Source citation: Market Analyst pattern (3 of 5 do not list ingredients fully), VoC theme 2
- Why this works: Reel version of the "Read the back" Instagram piece scheduled for May 15. Test paid version in parallel.

## Meta Ad 3
- Format: Static
- Hook: "Switched from Mamaearth because their ingredient list is half-empty marketing words."
- Primary text: {customer-G}, Pune. Real review, April 2026. We did not edit it. We did not ask permission to use it (we did, that's why this is here). She switched after she read both back labels. We make it easy.
- Headline: "What she read."
- Description: "Then she switched."
- CTA: Shop Now
- Image / video brief: Phone screenshot of the review text on Amazon, surrounded by white space. Bottle in lower-right.
- Source citation: VoC theme 2, {customer-G} verbatim
- Why this works: Names the competitor in the customer's voice (not ours). Higher CTR for switching ads. Legal-safer than naming the competitor ourselves.

## Meta Ad 4
- Format: Carousel (4 cards)
- Hook (card 1): "4 quotes from 4 customers. Same theme."
- Primary text: 4 unedited customer quotes on the same idea: we are the brand they wished existed because we printed the ingredient list. Each from a different city. None paid. All verified.
- Headline: "What our customers wrote."
- Description: "Their words. Not ours."
- CTA: Shop Now
- Image / video brief: 4-card carousel, each card is one quote on a soft background. Source label (city + month) in lower-right.
- Source citation: VoC theme 2, 4 verbatim quotes from {customer-C}, {customer-G}, {customer-H}, {customer-X}
- Why this works: Saves rate in baby category is highest on quote-carousels. Founder voice absent; customer voice central.

## Meta Ad 5
- Format: Static
- Hook: "We did not buy this review."
- Primary text: 14 of our 87 customer messages this month said some version of "I switched because I could actually read the ingredient list". We are the only brand in the category that has made ingredient transparency the explicit thesis. Read the back of any other bottle.
- Headline: "14 of 87."
- Description: "Same theme. Same close."
- CTA: Shop Now
- Image / video brief: 4-quote collage with the 14-of-87 number large in centre. Sources cited.
- Source citation: VoC report executive read, theme 2 frequency
- Why this works: Specific number ("14 of 87") is more credible than "most customers". Founder-grade depth ad.

## SAFETY FLAGS
- All customer quotes (Ad 1, Ad 3, Ad 4) must have signed permission to use in paid creative. Retention Manager should confirm.
- Ad 3 names "Mamaearth" in the customer's quoted text. Legally cleaner than us naming the competitor, but keep the screenshot raw (do not edit the quote) and store the original review URL on file.
- Ad 5 cites "14 of 87" — verify the exact count from VoC report before publish.
