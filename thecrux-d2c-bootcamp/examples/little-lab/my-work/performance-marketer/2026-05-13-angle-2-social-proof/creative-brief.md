# Creative Brief — Angle 2: Social Proof (Ingredient Transparency)
Date: 2026-05-13
For: design team + AI image tool

## The angle in one sentence
Real customer quotes, unedited, with sources, on a single theme: they switched because we printed the full ingredient list.

## Why now (data)
- VoC theme #2: "ingredient transparency is the reason to switch" — 14 of 87 (16%)
- Source: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 2
- Market Analyst signal: 3 of 5 competitors use "natural" or "toxin-free" as flagship. The wedge is open right now.

## The 3 must-haves in the visual
1. The customer quote rendered in legible body type, not stylised. Quotes are the asset; do not turn them into typography pieces.
2. Source citation in the corner: customer initial + city + month. Builds trust through specificity.
3. The bottle present but not central. The customer voice is the lead.

## Tone
- Voice always-list applies but the writing should foreground the customer's words, not the founder's. The customer-voice in our category sounds more like Class-10 reading age than Class-8.
- Voice never-list: do NOT use "natural", "100% safe", "premium", or any banned word, even in the surrounding copy.
- Reading age: Match the customer's actual register. Quotes are exact.
- Mood reference: New York Times "Reader Picks" comment design. Quiet, sourced, credible.

## What it must NOT show
- Edited customer quotes. Even minor grammar fixes. Use sic where needed.
- Customer faces or full names. Initials and city only.
- The brand wordmark at hero-size.
- Generic "happy customer" stock imagery. Use the actual review screenshot or quote layout.

## Examples to anchor (not copy)
- New York Times opinion column "Reader Picks" comment design for the source-citation layout
- Sebamed India's reviews-led IG carousels for the comparison framing (without the unsubstantiated medical claims)

## Deliverables
- 4 static quote cards, 1:1 and 4:5
- 1 reel storyboard for Ad 2 (the three-bottle comparison)
- 1 carousel set for Ad 4 (4 quotes)

## SAFETY FLAGS
- Permission to use each verbatim quote in paid creative must be on file before publish. Retention Manager confirms.
- Customer initials only; never full names. {customer-C}, {customer-G}, {customer-H}.
- If a quote names a competitor, keep the screenshot raw and store the URL of the original review on file in Ops Manager.
