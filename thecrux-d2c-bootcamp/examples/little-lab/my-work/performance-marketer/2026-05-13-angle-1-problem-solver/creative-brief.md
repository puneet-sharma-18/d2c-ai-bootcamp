# Creative Brief — Angle 1: Problem-solver (Cradle Cap)
Date: 2026-05-13
For: design team + AI image tool

## The angle in one sentence
The brand that cleared {customer-E}'s 8-month cradle cap in 4 days, by naming the active and the percentage on the bottle, when no other brand does either.

## Why now (data)
- VoC theme #1: "cradle cap actually worked" — 18 of 87 messages (21%), 7 unsolicited Amazon reviews
- Source: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 1
- Market Analyst signal: Mother Sparsh is preparing a cradle cap SKU launch; our window to own the conversation is narrowing

## The 3 must-haves in the visual
1. The bottle's back label visible enough to read "piroctone olamine 1% w/w". This is the single most important visual asset.
2. A real before/after of a baby's scalp, with parental consent, with the timeline (Day 1 / Day 4 / Day 7).
3. The brand wordmark in lower-right, no larger than the timeline text. The product is the lead, not the brand.

## Tone
- Voice always-list (CLAUDE.md Section 7): "Ingredient list, top to bottom" / "Paediatrically reviewed" / "Small batch" / "For the parent who reads the back of the bottle"
- Voice never-list: do NOT use "natural", "100% safe", "gentle", "toxin-free", "pure", "elevate", "unlock"
- Reading age: Class 8. Short sentences. Parents read this between feeds.
- Mood reference: clinical-warm. Think Aesop's spec-forward minimalism crossed with the founder-warmth of Sangini's Instagram early days.

## What it must NOT show
- Stock baby photos. Real customer kids only, with consent, first names only.
- The word "natural" anywhere in the creative.
- A comparison frame that names a specific competitor brand. "Category baseline" or "the typical bottle" is the safe wording.
- Stock paediatricians in white coats. If a paediatrician appears, they must be a real paediatric review panel member with signed release.

## Examples to anchor (not copy)
- The Honest Company's pre-pivot product education era (2014 to 2016) for the "name everything" tone
- Sebamed's clinical-cool packaging photography for the spec-forward shot composition

## Deliverables
- 1 hero static, 1:1 + 4:5 + 9:16
- 1 reel storyboard (the founder-on-camera + before/after timeline)
- 1 carousel set (5 cards: hook + 3 question + close)
- All images delivered with metadata: brand, SKU, angle name, source citation

## SAFETY FLAGS
- All "before/after" images need signed parental release on file at Ops before publish.
- "Day 4 / Day 7" timeline holds for most cases per VoC; a small percentage take longer. Footnote required on any creative that publishes the timeline.
