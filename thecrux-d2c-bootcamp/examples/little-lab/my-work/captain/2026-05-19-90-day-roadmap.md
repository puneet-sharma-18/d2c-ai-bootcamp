## The brand in one paragraph
Little Lab is a baby skincare brand at ARR ~₹4.2Cr (9 SKUs, 55% D2C, 30% Amazon), founded by Riya Shah. The brand thesis is ingredient transparency: every SKU lists every ingredient by name, the actives carry the percentage, and the paediatric review panel is named on the site. Primary persona is the Tier-1 metro mom 30-36 who reads the back of the bottle. Anti-positioning: never uses "natural", "toxin-free" or "100% safe". The Cradle Cap Balm 50ml is the trust-entry SKU.
