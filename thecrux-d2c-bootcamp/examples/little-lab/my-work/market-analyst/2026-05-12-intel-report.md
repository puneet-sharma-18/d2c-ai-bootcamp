## Executive read in 5 lines
1. Mother Sparsh launched a "Newborn Calendula Lotion" at ₹529 this week, undercutting our Newborn Daily Lotion (₹549) by 4% on a near-identical SKU. Our ingredient transparency angle still wins, but the price gap is now visible on the Amazon listing card.
2. We hold the upper-mid pricing band (₹449 to ₹599 across the 3 hero SKUs). Mamaearth sits ₹100 below us on shelf, Mother Sparsh sits ₹20 to ₹30 below, The Mom's Co matches us within ₹15. We are not premium; we are "transparency premium".
3. None of the 5 competitors publish full ingredient list percentages on their Amazon listings. 3 of 5 use the word "natural" in every product title. This is the content gap we can attack with a "Read the back of the bottle" Instagram series.
4. Mother Sparsh is running 14 active Meta ads, 9 of them retargeting D2C site abandoners. The Mom's Co is running 22 active ads, broad targeting. We are running 6. The paid channel we are absent from is YouTube pre-roll, where Mamaearth alone is spending ~₹15L/month per SimilarWeb signal.
5. Ship in the next 30 days: an Amazon A+ block on Cradle Cap Balm that names the active ingredient (1% piroctone olamine) and the % w/w. No competitor does this. It pre-empts Mother Sparsh's likely cradle cap launch.

## Per competitor

### Mother Sparsh
- **Pricing**: Newborn Calendula Lotion ₹529 (200ml), Baby Massage Oil ₹449 (200ml), Plant-powered Wipes ₹245 (72ct). Subscription is 10% off. First-order bundle drops effective price ~18%. Premium-leaning but discount-aggressive at first-buy.
- **Positioning**: "Ayurveda-inspired plant-powered baby care." Always-words: ayurveda, plant-powered, gentle, safe. Never talks about: full ingredient breakdown, specific actives, paediatric review process. Target customer reads as Tier-1 and Tier-2 mid-income mom, 26 to 36.
- **Content cadence**: Instagram 4 posts/week, mix of reel + carousel + static (2:1:1). YouTube 1 long-form/month, mostly recipe-style "how to use" walkthroughs. Blog updates ~2 posts/week, SEO-optimised on "newborn care" head terms. Engagement signal: comments 30 to 80 per post.
- **Organic**: Ranks page 1 for "ayurvedic baby massage oil", "plant-powered baby wipes". Press in last 90 days: YourStory Q1 feature on ₹100Cr ARR milestone, ET Now mention.
- **Paid**: 14 active Meta ads (Ad Library, checked 2026-05-12). 9 retargeting D2C abandoners. Creative leans into testimonial-style UGC. Estimated spend ₹40 to 60L/month based on impression-share signal. Sponsored Amazon listings on top 8 SKUs.
- **Where they beat us**: Brand awareness from 4 years of retail presence in Apollo + Tata 1mg. Ayurvedic narrative lands in Tier-2 where "ingredient list" does not (yet) resonate.
- **Where we beat them**: We publish the full ingredient list with proportions. They say "plant-powered ayurvedic blend" and stop. The technical parent (our primary persona) reads that as marketing language.
- **Source links**: mothersparsh.com/products/newborn-calendula-lotion, Amazon ASIN check on 2026-05-12, Meta Ad Library at facebook.com/ads/library?id=mothersparsh.

### The Mom's Co
- **Pricing**: Natural Baby Wash ₹549 (300ml), Natural Baby Lotion ₹599 (200ml), Natural Baby Massage Oil ₹495 (200ml). Marico-owned since 2022. Stable pricing, no discount-heavy first-buy push. Bundle discount ~12%.
- **Positioning**: "Toxin-free baby care backed by science." Always-words: toxin-free, dermatologically-tested, mother-approved, safe-for-sensitive-skin. Never talks about: specific surfactants, exact actives, what "toxin-free" means in chemistry terms.
- **Content cadence**: Instagram 5 posts/week, very polished, agency-feel. YouTube 2 long-form/month, doctor-led explainer style. Blog 1 post/week, SEO on "toxin-free baby" cluster. Engagement signal: 50 to 120 comments, but high bot-feel ratio.
- **Organic**: Ranks page 1 for "toxin-free baby products", "natural baby wash India". Page 2 for "newborn lotion no fragrance" where we rank page 1. Press in last 90 days: BW Disrupt, Inc42 piece on Marico's portfolio.
- **Paid**: 22 active Meta ads. Broad targeting (Tier-1 mom 25 to 40). Google Shopping on every SKU. Sponsored Amazon listings on top 12 SKUs. Estimated spend ₹80L to 1Cr/month.
- **Where they beat us**: Distribution depth (offline + Amazon + Flipkart + quick commerce simultaneously). Ad spend at 3x ours. Marico-backed credibility for retail buyers.
- **Where we beat them**: "Toxin-free" is the exact phrase our CLAUDE.md bans because it is a marketing word without chemistry definition. Our ingredient transparency is the answer to the question their term avoids.
- **Source links**: themomsco.com/collections/baby-care, Amazon ASIN check, Meta Ad Library.

### Mylo
- **Pricing**: Mylo brand baby care is in the lower-mid band (₹299 to ₹449 across baby skincare). They lead with the parenting app, products are pull-along. App is free, mom community is 800K+.
- **Positioning**: "From a parent, for parents." Always-words: trusted, community, expert-backed. App-and-community-first, product-second. Adjacent rather than direct.
- **Content cadence**: App-native first. Instagram is broadcast-only for the app, not product-led. ~3 posts/week.
- **Organic**: Ranks for "baby care app India" and parenting head terms. Product SEO is thin.
- **Paid**: Meta ads heavy on app install (~30 active). Product ads ~8 active. App-first funnel.
- **Where they beat us**: App engagement creates a moat we cannot match on product alone. Mom community trust ramps SKU-2 conversion fast.
- **Where we beat them**: We are tighter on skincare specifically. A mom looking for a cradle cap balm finds us first because we have the only category-specific SKU with the active named.
- **Source links**: mylofamily.com, Play Store listing.

### R for Rabbit
- **Pricing**: Skincare in the ₹399 to ₹549 band. Cross-sells with strollers, car seats, breast pumps. Skincare is ~15% of revenue per their 2024 IPO DRHP filing.
- **Positioning**: "Safe + smart gear and care for the new parent." Always-words: safe, smart, premium. Hardware-led story, skincare is secondary.
- **Content cadence**: Instagram 4 posts/week, gear-heavy. Skincare gets ~1 post/week.
- **Organic**: Ranks for "baby stroller India", "baby car seat India". Skincare SEO weak.
- **Paid**: Meta ads ~12 active, 10 on gear. Skincare ads ~2 active.
- **Where they beat us**: Distribution + parent loyalty from gear purchases that pre-date a skincare buy. A stroller customer is already in the funnel.
- **Where we beat them**: We do not sell strollers and we will not. The technical parent sees the gear-first brand and looks for a specialist; that is us.
- **Source links**: rforrabbit.com, DRHP filing summary.

### Mamaearth (Honasa)
- **Pricing**: Whole-family natural brand. Baby skincare is ~25% of revenue. Pricing ₹349 to ₹499 across baby SKUs. Aggressive on quick commerce and modern trade.
- **Positioning**: "Goodness inside." Always-words: natural, toxin-free, made-safe-certified. Mass-market natural.
- **Content cadence**: Heavy. Instagram 7 posts/week. YouTube 4 long-form/month. Blog daily. Influencer collabs continuous.
- **Organic**: Ranks page 1 for "natural baby care", "toxin-free baby wash", "best baby lotion India". Strong SEO across head and long-tail.
- **Paid**: 30+ active Meta ads. Google Shopping on every SKU. YouTube pre-roll ~₹15L/month (SimilarWeb signal). The widest paid presence in the category.
- **Where they beat us**: Awareness, ad spend at 10x ours, retail shelf width.
- **Where we beat them**: They use "natural" and "toxin-free" as flagship words. Both are on our CLAUDE.md ban list. Our ingredient transparency is the wedge against the largest player.
- **Source links**: mamaearth.in, Meta Ad Library, Honasa Q4 FY26 investor deck.

## Patterns across the set
- **What 3 of the 5 are doing that we are not**: YouTube pre-roll spend at scale. Mamaearth, Mother Sparsh and The Mom's Co are all there; we are absent. The pre-roll format suits the "Read the back of the bottle" educational angle.
- **What 1 of the 5 is doing that none of the others are**: Mother Sparsh is testing UGC-style testimonial ads with real moms (not influencer-style). 9 of 14 of their ads are first-person testimonials. Highest CTR creative format in the category right now.
- **The category narrative that is shifting**: "Natural" and "toxin-free" are softening as differentiators because every brand uses them. The next narrative is specificity (named ingredients, named percentages, named test reports). We are 18 months early on it; the window is open.
