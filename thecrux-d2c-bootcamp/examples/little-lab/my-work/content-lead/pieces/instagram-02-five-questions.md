# Instagram Carousel 02 — 5 questions to ask before you buy any baby lotion

**Channel**: Instagram Carousel (6 slides)
**Calendar slot**: Day 6, May 18 Sun
**Theme served**: Ingredient transparency #2 (VoC)
**Source**: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 2

---

## Slide 1 (hook)
**5 questions to ask before you buy any baby lotion.**
Flip the bottle. Then ask these.

## Slide 2
**1. How many ingredients are listed?**
Anything under 8 means something is being hidden behind a category word ("ayurvedic blend", "plant extracts"). Ours has 11. Each is named.

## Slide 3
**2. Are any percentages on the label?**
The regulator allows brands to publish percentages of actives. Most don't. Without a percentage, "infused with chamomile" can mean 0.001%. Our actives carry % w/w.

## Slide 4
**3. What are the preservatives?**
Almost every baby lotion has preservatives. The question is which. "Food-grade preservatives" is not an answer. "Sodium benzoate 0.4%, potassium sorbate 0.2%" is.

## Slide 5
**4. Is the active named?**
For problem-solver SKUs (cradle cap, diaper rash, eczema-friendly), the active matters. If the bottle says "natural blend" instead of naming the active, you cannot ask your paediatrician whether it's the right active for your kid.

## Slide 6
**5. Who reviewed it?**
"Dermatologically tested" needs a lab report ID. "Paediatrician approved" needs a name. We list the paediatric review panel on every SKU page. If a brand does not, ask why.

## Slide 7 (CTA)
**Read the back. Then decide.**
Link in bio takes you to our full ingredient list for every SKU.

**Why this works**: Carousels with numbered questions are the highest-save format on Instagram. Each question maps directly to a VoC theme. The brand's "always" phrases ("paediatrically reviewed", "ingredient list") show up naturally. The CTA is soft and brand-consistent.
