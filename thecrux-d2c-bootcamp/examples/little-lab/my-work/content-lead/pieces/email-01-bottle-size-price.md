# Email 01 — ₹549 vs ₹329: the bottle size is the price difference

**Channel**: Email (campaign send)
**Calendar slot**: Day 2, May 14 Wed
**Theme served**: Price objection theme #4 (VoC)
**Source**: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 4

---

## Subject line
The most-asked question this month (about our price)

## Preheader
And why the cheaper bottle is not actually cheaper.

## Body

Hi {first_name},

The question we got the most this month from new buyers:

"₹549 feels steep. Mother Sparsh is ₹529. Why?"

Fair question. Let me show you the math.

**The 200ml Newborn Daily Lotion lasts ~45 days at twice-daily application on a 3-month-old**. That works out to ₹12 a day. The ₹329 brand at 100ml lasts ~22 days at the same use. That is ₹15 a day.

You are not paying more per day with us. You are paying for a bigger bottle, fewer re-order trips, and an ingredient list that does not change SKU to SKU.

That is the surface answer. The deeper one is this: we print every ingredient at every percentage we are allowed to publish. Two of our competitors do not. When you read the back of our bottle and theirs, you will see the difference. We are not asking you to take that on trust; we are asking you to flip the bottle.

If price is genuinely the blocker, we have a 100ml trial size launching in June. Reply to this email with "TRIAL" and you'll be on the list.

Best
Riya
Founder, Little Lab

P.S. If you have already bought from us once and are due to re-order, hit reply and I'll send you a 12% bundle code for your second SKU. Returning customer perk.

---

**Why this works**: Names the exact competitor and price the VoC data showed customers comparing us to ({customer-L}). Reframes "expensive" as "per-day cost", a frame the technical parent persona reads in their own head anyway. Closes with a real next-step (the 100ml trial that is on the product roadmap).

**CTA option**: Hard ask is "reply TRIAL". Soft ask is "flip the bottle next time you're at the store".
