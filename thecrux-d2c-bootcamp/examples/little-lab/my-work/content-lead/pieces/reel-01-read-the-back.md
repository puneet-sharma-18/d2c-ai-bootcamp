# Instagram Reel 01 — Read the back of any baby lotion

**Channel**: Instagram Reel (30 seconds)
**Calendar slot**: Day 3, May 15 Thu
**Theme served**: Anti-positioning wedge / Ingredient transparency
**Source**: CLAUDE.md anti-positioning + Market Analyst pattern (3 of 5 competitors do not list full ingredients)

---

## Hook (0:00 to 0:03)
ON CAMERA, founder Riya holding three baby lotion bottles, ours and two competitors:
**"I am going to read you the back of three baby lotions. Tell me which one you'd buy."**

## Beat 1 (0:03 to 0:10)
Holds up competitor 1 (label blurred but framing suggests Mamaearth).
**"Ingredients: 'aqua, plant-based extracts, natural oils, ayurvedic blend, preservatives'. Five words. That is the ingredient list."**

## Beat 2 (0:10 to 0:17)
Holds up competitor 2 (Mother Sparsh).
**"This one has 'calendula extract, ayurvedic herbs, gentle plant actives'. Three words. That is the ingredient list."**

## Beat 3 (0:17 to 0:25)
Holds up Little Lab Newborn Daily Lotion. Camera zooms into the back label.
**"This one has eleven ingredients. Each one named. Three of them have the percentage. The active is at the bottom because the regulator says we have to list by descending order. That is the ingredient list."**

## Close (0:25 to 0:30)
ON CAMERA, holding up our bottle.
**"This is the only one I would put on my own kid. Not because it's mine. Because I can read it."**

## On-screen text overlay throughout
"#ReadTheBackOfTheBottle"

## Caption (under the reel)
Three baby lotions. Three back labels. One of them tells you what is actually in the bottle. The other two ask you to trust a marketing word. We picked the harder route on purpose. Comment which one you'd pick.

---

**Why this works**: Hits the Market Analyst observation (3 of 5 competitors do not list full ingredients) as a visual. The founder-on-camera format is the highest-CTR creative right now per Mother Sparsh's testimonial-style ads (the format winning in the category, per Market Analyst report). No invented competitor names — the visual evidence is the label, and labels are public.

**CTA option**: Soft CTA via the comment prompt. No hard sell. The brand voice rule is "the ingredient list is the close".

## SAFETY FLAGS
- The visual implies a side-by-side comparison with named competitors. Legal pass needed before publish. Specifically: do not say competitor names in the audio, only show their labels in frame at a distance that is identifiable but not zoomed-in.
- "Eleven ingredients" claim must match exactly what is on our current bottle. If formulation changed, re-verify.
