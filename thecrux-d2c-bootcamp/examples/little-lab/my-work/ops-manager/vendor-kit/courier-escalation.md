# Vendor Email — Courier Escalation (Tier-2 SLA)
Use when: 3+ tickets in a 7-day window citing delayed delivery from the same hub / pin-code prefix.
Tone: professional, direct, founder-grade. Vendor-side reads in second language often. Short sentences.

## Subject lines (3 variants)
1. Urgent: SLA breach on {hub} routes, last 7 days
2. Need a call this week on {hub} performance vs SLA
3. Service review: {hub} routes, action needed

## Body

Hi {account-manager-name},

Writing to flag a pattern in the last 7 days on the {hub} hub.

We have logged {N} support tickets from customers in the {pin-code-range} range citing delivery times of 7 to 9 days. Our SLA per the December 2024 contract is 4 days, max 5 days. Three of these customers compared our delivery time unfavourably to a named competitor brand, so this is a customer-experience problem now, not just an internal metric.

Specifically:
- Order {order-id} placed {date}, delivered {date}, transit {N days}
- Order {order-id} placed {date}, delivered {date}, transit {N days}
- Order {order-id} placed {date}, delivered {date}, transit {N days}

Two questions for the response:
1. What was the root cause for these three orders specifically? Hub backup, sortation issue, last-mile partner? Please name it.
2. What is the corrective action plan for the next 14 days? Is there a service-tier change we should consider for this lane?

I would like to get on a call this Thursday or Friday to align on the response and the lane economics. Can your team propose a time?

Best
Riya
Founder, Little Lab

## What to attach
- The three order IDs as a CSV with date placed, date delivered, transit days
- Screenshot of the customer ticket exchange (with PII redacted) for the most-comparative ticket

## What NOT to write
- Do not threaten contract termination in writing on the first escalation. That is a phone call, after the corrective action plan is on the table.
- Do not name the competitor brand in writing. "A named competitor brand" is sufficient and legally cleaner.
- Do not include a customer phone number, full name, or address. Use the order ID only.

## If they do not reply within 3 days
- Phone call to the account manager. If they do not pick up, escalate to their VP per the contract escalation matrix.
- Move the SOP "Tier-2 Delivery Friction Response" Step 5 decision deadline forward.
