# Positioning, The Paan Legacy

The Paan Legacy is a premium D2C gourmet paan and mukhwas brand for Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian families (32 to 48, household income ₹15L+) who want the after-meal ritual back without the tobacco, the supari or the stain on the wall. Every paan is hand-rolled, named-ingredient, tobacco-free and meant to be eaten, not spat. Voice is warm, heritage-rooted and modern. Customers come back monthly (ritual) and seasonally (gifting at Diwali, weddings, corporate Diwali bonuses). The wedge is category-specific: we are paan-and-mukhwas-only, not a sweet shop that also sells paan; we list named ingredients and roast in-house, not bulk-source; packaging is gift-ready without being asked. We compete with Bombay Sweet Shop on the premium-Indian-sweets adjacency, with Anjali Mukhwas on traditional mukhwas, with Karachi Bakery on legacy-gifting recall and with the loose paan-wala on the lapsed ritual. We never compete on price (loose paan is ₹15, we are ₹179 to ₹299). We never use the words "gourmet", "artisanal", "premium" or "authentic" in copy; the category has cheapened them. FSSAI compliance is non-negotiable.
