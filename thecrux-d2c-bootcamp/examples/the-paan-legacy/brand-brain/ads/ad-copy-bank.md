## What is missing

- A creative that speaks to the corporate gifting buyer (HR persona). October-November push.
- A creative for the curious health-modern persona that does not start with Granola Goodness.
- A summer-shipping reassurance creative (cold pack, insulated box) to convert hesitant Delhi / Hyderabad / Lucknow viewers.

## What we will not run (voice rules)

- "Gourmet paan" or "artisanal paan" creative. Banned.
- "Premium" headline as a stand-alone word.
- Any "elevate", "unlock" or "in today's fast-paced world" copy. AI tells, fails brand sniff test.
- Health claims beyond "traditionally taken after meals". FSSAI claim wall.
