# Competitors, The Paan Legacy

One line per competitor explaining why they matter. Market Analyst in Session 2 expands these with real intel; this is the starting set.

- **Bombay Sweet Shop** (bombaysweetshop.com): Direct premium-Indian-sweets adjacent, Mumbai-strong, founder-known; treats paan as one SKU of many, which is where our category specialisation cuts in.
- **Anjali Mukhwas** (anjalimukhwas.com / retail): Direct mukhwas competitor, Gujarat and Mumbai distribution, traditional bulk-sourced, ₹130 to ₹180 price band; we beat them on named ingredients, hand-roasting and gift-ready packaging.
- **Karachi Bakery (online)** (karachibakery.com): Legacy gifting brand with strong South India brand recall and festival ad spend; not paan-focused, so our category-specialist positioning differentiates.
- **Local paan-wala (the default)**: The lapsed paan ritual's incumbent. Ubiquitous, ₹15-30, instant; we beat them on tobacco-free, hygiene, brand and gifting, and we will never beat them on price or location.
- **Open Secret / Tagz Foods** (opensecret.in / tagzfoods.com): Adjacent premium snack D2C; their D2C playbook depth and Meta spend are the bar we measure our own paid ops against; different occasion (snack vs post-meal ritual), so direct customer overlap is small.
