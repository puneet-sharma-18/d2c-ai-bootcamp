# Unit Economics, The Paan Legacy

Last refreshed by Puneet on 2026-05-12. Numbers below are the founder's working set, drawn from a 90-day Shopify pull, partner dashboards (Zomato, Swiggy), Meta and Google ad accounts and our own monthly P&L. They get re-stated every Monday at the brief. Where I hedge, I say so.

## Headline

We are running at roughly ₹30L revenue / month blended, growing 3 to 6% week on week through April and early May 2026. Blended gross margin sits in the high 50s. Blended CAC is over target this month, mostly because Meta cost per result climbed in late April and we have not yet rotated creative. Repeat purchase rate is steady at 30%, which is the single healthiest number on this page.

## LTV

I do not have a true 12-month cohort yet. My Shopify MCP pulls 90 days, and my paid channels do not give me cohort-level revenue back. The numbers below are estimates I am willing to defend in a Monday meeting; they need a real cohort pull to harden.

| Cut | LTV (₹) | How I got there |
|---|---|---|
| Blended, 12-month estimate | 1,180 | AOV ₹734 × 1.6 orders/year × 1.0 (no haircut applied yet for refunds) |
| Gross-profit LTV | 700 | Above × 59% gross margin |
| LTV : CAC ratio (target) | 2.9x | ₹1,180 / ₹400 |
| LTV : CAC ratio (current) | 2.5x | ₹1,180 / ₹475 |

The 1.6 orders/year assumption comes from a hand-pulled cohort of 200 customers acquired in November 2025, tracked through April 2026 (~5 months). Annualised gives 1.6. I am hedging on this because the ritual buyer persona is monthly and the gifting buyer is seasonal, and the mix shifts across the year. A real 12-month cohort pull is on the Q3 task list.

## Notes for the founder (where I am hedging)

- LTV is the weakest number on this page. Real 12-month cohort needs a Shopify export beyond 90 days. Until then, treat the ₹1,180 as a working estimate.
- CAC blended uses new-customer count from Shopify, but a fraction of those came in through Zomato and Swiggy (where we cannot reliably tell new from repeat at the customer level because the partner dashboards do not give us a customer ID). I am assigning all Zomato + Swiggy sponsored orders as "new" by default; that may overstate the new pool by 10 to 15%.
- Gross margin per SKU is from the operations sheet last updated in March 2026. Saffron prices moved up in April, so Almond Delight's 64% may be 1 to 2 points lower this quarter.
- Channel mix excludes franchise revenue from the digital base. When the standing report rolls in offline, the mix will read differently.
- Repeat rate is computed on order count, not customer count. Two orders from one customer in 7 days count as one new + one repeat. A customer-count version of this would read slightly lower.
- The summer melt risk on Almond Delight and Dry Fruit Meetha Paan affects the next two months. If returns spike, gross margin on those SKUs takes a 3 to 5 point hit and the CAC math worsens.
