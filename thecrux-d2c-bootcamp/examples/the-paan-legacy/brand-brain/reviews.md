## Themes the founder is watching

- "Eat, do not spit" is the single most-repeated promise customers verify before and after purchase.
- "Grandmother's paan" / "my mother's house" — heritage language shows up unprompted across personas and cities.
- Summer melt risk on Almond Delight and Dry Fruit Meetha Paan (Delhi, Hyderabad, Lucknow May to August) — known weakness, customers raise it before we do.
- Mukhwas pack size flagged twice as small for the price; revisit pack sizing this quarter.
- Repeat buyers (3+ orders) explicitly asking for subscription / reminder; demand exists before the feature.
