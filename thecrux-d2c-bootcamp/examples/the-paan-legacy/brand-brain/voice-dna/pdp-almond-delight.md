## Long description

Almond Delight is the paan we send to a wedding when we want the bride's family to remember us. Almonds slivered by hand the morning of the roll. Rose petals from the Pushkar belt. Saffron, three threads per paan, the Kashmiri grade. Gulkand from a small batch we put up every March. Built on the meetha base, finished with a thin silver leaf for the special-occasion ones.

This is not the everyday paan. It is the one you eat at the end of a Sunday lunch when the in-laws are over, or the one you send instead of a sweet box at Diwali. Two paans per person is the right number; more and the saffron stops being a luxury and starts being a routine.

## What it tastes like

The almond goes first, the rose comes second, the saffron sits at the back. The leaf is leaf, not garnish. You will swallow it; that is the point. If anyone tells you to spit, they have never eaten this version.
