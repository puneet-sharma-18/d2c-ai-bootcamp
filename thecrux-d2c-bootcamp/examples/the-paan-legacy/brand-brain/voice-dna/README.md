# voice-dna/

Five to ten samples of writing that *sounds like the brand*. PDP body copy, post-purchase emails, social posts, packaging inserts, founder LinkedIn / Instagram caption, WhatsApp replies. The fewer source files we have to interpret what "The Paan Legacy voice" is, the more later teammates default to generic D2C tone. The point of this folder is to remove that ambiguity.

Every later session reads from here:

- Brand Brain (Session 1) re-extracts "always" and "never" phrases here if the voice test scores under 4.
- Content Lead (Session 4) mimics this tone in the 30-day calendar and pieces.
- Performance Marketer (Session 6) re-uses winning hooks for ad copy.
- Storefront (Session 7) leans on PDP samples here for the rewrites.

## Voice fingerprint, in three sentences

We sound like a heritage food founder talking to a curious adult who already knows what paan is. We use specific words ("hand-roasted", "named ingredients", "rose, gulkand, fennel, cardamom") and never abstract ones ("gourmet", "artisanal", "premium", "authentic"). We assume the reader is smart, smells AI tells from across the room, and will close the tab the moment a sentence starts with "In today's fast-paced world".
