## Long description

This is the paan you grew up watching the adults of the house eat after Sunday lunch. Rose, gulkand, fennel, cardamom, a thread of coconut. Hand-rolled in small batches. Eat and swallow. No tobacco. No supari. No spitting into a sink.

We made the Meetha Paan because the local paan-wala stopped making this version a long time ago. The category traded a 2,000-year-old after-meal habit for a tobacco hit and a stain on the wall. Meetha Paan is the version your grandmother would still recognise.

## What you get

- Six paan, individually wrapped, in a kraft box
- Best within 14 days of dispatch; eats best cold
- Vegetarian. No tobacco, no supari, no zarda. Allergens: cardamom, rose, fennel

## How to eat it

Take one out, eat it in one piece. Do not bite and put down. Do not spit. The leaf is meant to be swallowed. If it is your first time, eat half and drink water.

## Storage

Keep refrigerated after delivery. If you live in Delhi, Hyderabad or Lucknow between May and August, ask us to ship in the insulated box (no extra cost, mention "summer shipping" in the order notes).
