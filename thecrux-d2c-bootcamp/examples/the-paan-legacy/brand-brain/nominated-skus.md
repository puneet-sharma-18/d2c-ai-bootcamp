# Nominated SKUs for the Workshop

Five SKUs picked for the later sessions (Content Lead, Performance Marketer, Storefront, Marketplace Editor). Pick reflects revenue weight, customer journey position and the moments we want to win for the rest of the year.

| # | SKU | Why nominated |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meetha Paan (TPL-MTH-001, ₹179) | The entry SKU. Largest unit volume. Most reviews. First taste a curious customer has of the brand. Content Lead and Performance Marketer cycle most of the acquisition copy through this. |
| 2 | Almond Delight (TPL-ALM-001, ₹299) | Wedding-and-gifting hero. Highest AOV per unit, second highest review count, the SKU that justifies the brand's premium positioning in real customer language. |
| 3 | Dry Fruit Meetha Paan (TPL-DFM-001, ₹259) | The "festival in a box". Diwali and wedding-season volume driver. Critical for the corporate gifting persona that lives 4 months a year. |
| 4 | Granola Goodness (TPL-GRN-001, ₹239) | Acquisition SKU for the curious health-modern persona. Ramps customers into the rest of the catalogue over 60 days. Content Lead needs to write for this audience differently than the family ritual buyer. |
| 5 | Herbal Mukhwas (TPL-MKW-001, ₹219) | Mukhwas anchor. Different occasion (digestion-focused, post-meal everywhere), different ingredient story (hand-roasted, named ingredients vs bulk-sourced category default). Tests whether voice carries across the paan / mukhwas split. |

## Landing page nomination

For the Storefront + Performance Marketer sessions, the landing page we will workshop is:

**https://thepaanlegacy.com/products/almond-delight**

Why this page: it is the SKU we send most paid traffic to, the AOV impact is largest, and the current PDP leans heavily on "gourmet" / "premium" wording which our voice rules flag as banned. Reworking this PDP fixes the largest gap between brand voice and customer experience.
