## Description (~190 words)
A meetha paan layered with three named dry fruits: cashew, slivered almond, raisin. Each paan carries a counted fill, because most paan in this category puts in dust and calls it "premium". We did not want to do that.

The base is our classic meetha (rose petals, slow-cooked gulkand, hand-roasted fennel, hand-cracked cardamom). The fill is the festival layer. Tobacco-free, supari-free, eat-and-swallow.

Box of 6, ₹259. Hand-roasted that week. Small batch. Named ingredients on the box.

FSSAI License #{number}. Suitable from age 6+. Tobacco-free.

Allergen note: contains tree nuts (almond, cashew). Confirmed allergen, not cross-contact. If you or anyone in your home is anaphylactic to cashew, please order our Almond Delight or Meetha Paan instead, both of which are cashew-free.

Summer note: from June to August, we hold back this SKU from pin codes forecast above 38°C and offer Almond Delight as a no-cost swap. We will email you if your address triggers the rule.

Order on Swiggy for same-day dessert-section delivery in metro pin codes.
