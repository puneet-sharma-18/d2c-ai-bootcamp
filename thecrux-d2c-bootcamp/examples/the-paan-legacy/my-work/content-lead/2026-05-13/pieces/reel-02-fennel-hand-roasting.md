## Caption (under the reel)
There are two ways to make mukhwas. Buy 50 kilos of fennel from a wholesaler and pack it into pouches. Or roast 2 kilos at a time and watch the colour change with your eyes.

We pick the second one because the wholesaler version is bitter by week three and most loose mukhwas at sweet shops is from week six.

Hand-roasted. Small batch. Dated on the jar before it goes into the pack.

- "Most loose mukhwas at sweet shops is from week six" is a competitive claim. Soften to "loose mukhwas at a sweet shop sits open for weeks" if the week-six specific cannot be substantiated.
- "A digestive doctor would actually approve" is a soft health-adjacent line drawn from CLAUDE.md USP for this SKU. Within compliance, but Ops review for FSSAI tone.
