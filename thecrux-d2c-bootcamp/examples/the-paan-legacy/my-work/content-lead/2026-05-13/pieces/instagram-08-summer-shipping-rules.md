## Image brief

## On-image text (the 4 rules)

**Rule 1.** Dispatch days May to September: Monday and Tuesday only outside the metros. We do not start a parcel on a Friday in summer.

**Rule 2.** Pin codes with forecast 32°C+ between dispatch and delivery: shipped in an insulated box. No extra charge.

**Rule 3.** Dry Fruit Meetha Paan and any chocolate-adjacent SKU: held back from 38°C+ pin codes between June and August. We will email you and offer Almond Delight as a swap.

**Rule 4.** Granola Goodness travels best. Almond Delight is the second-best summer ship. Meetha Paan ships everywhere. Mukhwas ships everywhere.

## Caption (under the post)
Last summer we had 6 customers write in about a melted gulkand or a soft granola edge. Most were in non-metro pin codes shipped on a Friday. Our fault.

These four rules go into our shipping screen from June 1. Save the image. If you are about to order to a hot city, this is what we will do at our end.

If a box arrives off, message us. We re-ship.
