Text:
"Q: Is this a regular paan that I spit out, or do I eat it?"
"A: You eat it. The whole thing. No tobacco, no supari, nothing that needs to come back out. The paan was originally a digestive, swallowed. We are putting it back."

## Slide 3 - Question 2 (price)
Text:
"Q: My corner paan-wala is ₹25. Why is yours ₹179?"
"A: Slivered almonds, rose petals, hand-roasted fennel, gulkand, saffron thread, betel leaf sourced within 36 hours. The ₹25 paan is mostly catechu paste and tobacco. The two are not the same product."

## Slide 4 - Question 3 (allergens)
Text:
"Q: My husband is anaphylactic to cashew. Is cashew in everything?"
"A: Almond Delight and Meetha Paan are cashew-free. Dry Fruit Meetha Paan and Granola Goodness contain cashew. The Masala Cranberry shares production line with cashew SKUs; we declare cross-contact possible. Full allergen list on the SKU page."

## Slide 5 - Question 4 (kids)
Text:
"Q: My nephew is 8. Can he eat one?"
"A: Yes, age 6+. No tobacco, no supari, no banned ingredients. The paan does contain tree nuts and the cardamom is a strong flavour at that age. Start with a half-paan."

## Slide 6 - Question 5 (shipping)
Text:
"Q: I am in Hyderabad in May. Will it arrive intact?"
"A: From May to September, we ship in insulated boxes to non-metro pin codes and we move all dispatches to Monday or Tuesday. Almond Delight ships best in summer. Dry Fruit Meetha Paan is held back from 32-degree pin codes for now. Honest answer."
