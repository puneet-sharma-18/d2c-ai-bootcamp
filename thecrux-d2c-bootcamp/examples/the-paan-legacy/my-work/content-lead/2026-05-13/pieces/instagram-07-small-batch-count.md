## Caption (under the carousel)
We have been calling our paan "small batch" since Day 1. People asked what we meant. Here it is, counted.

The reason for the counted answer is that "small batch" is a word the food industry has worn out. We did not want to be in that category. So the answer is a number, not an adjective.
