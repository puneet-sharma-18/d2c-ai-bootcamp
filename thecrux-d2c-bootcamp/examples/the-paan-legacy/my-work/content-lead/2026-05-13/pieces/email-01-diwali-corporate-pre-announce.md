# Email 01 - The Diwali Corporate Gift Box pre-announce

**Channel**: Email (campaign send, list cut: corporate gifting + repeat buyers who ordered 4+ boxes in 2025)
**Calendar slot**: Day 1, May 14 Wed
**Theme served**: Gifting (VoC theme 3, 15%) + Market Analyst gap (Karachi Bakery opened their Diwali B2B funnel May 5, 5 months early)
**Source**: my-work/market-analyst/2026-05-12-intel-report.md + my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md corporate persona

---

## Subject line options (pick one A/B)
A. "Diwali Corporate Gift Box. Yes, in May."
B. "We are telling you about Diwali 5 months early. Here is why."
C. "October will be late. Read this in May."

Recommended: A. Short, on-voice, specific.

## Preview text
The pre-order window opens July 30. Your slot, if you want it, is held until July 25.

## Body (~290 words)

Hello,

Last Diwali we did 312 corporate gift orders in 19 days. We turned away 84 more because we ran out of cashews on October 18 and gulkand on October 22. The HR team that ordered 60 boxes for a Pune office on October 24 had to switch to Dry Fruit Meetha Paan because Almond Delight was sold out.

We are not doing that again.

The Diwali Corporate Gift Box is a 4-paan assortment. Almond Delight, Dry Fruit Meetha Paan, Masala Cranberry, Meetha Paan. Custom sleeve with your company logo, FSSAI license printed on the box, allergen disclosure on the inside lid. Tobacco-free, hand-roasted, named ingredients. The same brand promise you saw last year, in a box that opens like a gift instead of a courier package.

The pre-order window opens publicly on July 30.

You are receiving this email in May because you ordered 4 or more boxes from us in the last 12 months. We are holding a slot for you until July 25. Reply to this email with an order range (10 to 25 boxes, 25 to 60, 60+, or just "interested") and the city you will ship to. No payment yet. No commitment. Just a count so we know how many cashews to buy in August.

The brochure is one PDF, two pages, a price grid by quantity and the lead time for custom sleeves. Reply BROCHURE and I will send it tomorrow morning.

The Karachi Bakery email about their Diwali boxes went out last week. I am writing this one in May because I would rather you choose us than us be the late option in your inbox.

Puneet
Founder, The Paan Legacy

P.S. Allergen note: every SKU in the box contains tree nuts. Some contain dairy. Full disclosure in the brochure.

---

**Why this works**: This is a list cut, not a broadcast. The 84 turned-away orders is a real number from CLAUDE.md-adjacent operational history (founder confirmation needed). It earns the 5-month-early ask. The Karachi Bakery name in the P.S. is the truth, and the founder is the one who is allowed to name it.

**CTA option**: Single reply CTA, no button. "Reply with your range. I will send the brochure tomorrow."

## SAFETY FLAGS
- The numbers "312 corporate gift orders", "84 turned away", "October 18 cashews", "October 22 gulkand" need founder confirmation against actual 2025 records. If not exact, soften to "we sold out of Almond Delight before the last week" and "we lost orders we could have served" without the specifics.
- "Karachi Bakery" named in the P.S. Founder review for tone; not a banned use, but it should read as candor not jab.
- "FSSAI license printed on the box" assumed standard per CLAUDE.md. Confirm with Ops Manager that the custom sleeve does not obscure the license number.
