## Caption (under the carousel)
The hardest part of writing a paan ingredient list is not the writing. It is the naming. Most paan SKUs say "natural flavours" or "spices" and stop there. We name the supplier region, the batch size and the roast week. Read the box before you read the brand.

Six ingredients. Counted. The seventh is the betel leaf and we list that on the SKU page too.

## SAFETY FLAGS
- "From a Pushkar-region supplier since 2022" needs founder confirmation. If the supplier is different, swap the region name. The specificity is the point; do not generalise to "premium supplier".
- "One thread per paan" is a quality claim. If the actual fill is "one to two threads", say so. Do not round up.
- "No added preservative beyond what the sugar does naturally" is a soft claim. Confirm with Ops that the gulkand supplier ingredient declaration matches. If a preservative is added, change to "no E202, no E211" and name what is and is not in.
