## Image brief
Plain off-white background. Six SKU thumbnails arranged in two rows of three. Below each thumbnail, a clean allergen tag in small caps: TREE NUTS, DAIRY, SESAME, etc. No decorative type. The image is supposed to be screenshotted and shared.

## Text on image (top, small)
"Allergen check. Every SKU. Saved on our profile under ALLERGENS."

- **Masala Cranberry**: Tree nuts (almond, cashew possible cross-contact), seeds (fennel). No dairy.

## Caption (under the post)
We were getting the same WhatsApp question two ways a week. "Cashew in this one? My husband is anaphylactic." "Is the Granola Goodness gluten-free? My son is celiac."

Here is every allergen on every SKU, in one image. Save it. Share it with the friend who asked you. If you have a specific question that is not here, reply to this post or message us. We will not deflect.

Granola Goodness is not gluten-free. The oats are oats. We do not pretend they are not.

Full ingredient list lives on every SKU page on the site. FSSAI license number on every box.

**Why this works**: Six WhatsApp pre-purchase questions in 30 days asked about allergens. The single-image format is built to be screenshotted and forwarded. The "we will not deflect" line earns trust faster than a perfect allergen statement does.

**CTA option**: "Save this image. Or share it with the person who asked you."

## SAFETY FLAGS
- Cashew cross-contact in Masala Cranberry needs Ops Manager verification. If the production line shares equipment with cashew SKUs, the disclosure is correct; if not, remove "cross-contact possible".
- Granola Goodness "sesame in some batches" needs verification. If sesame is always in, say "always contains sesame". If never in, remove. The "some batches" wording is a compliance grey zone.
- "We will not deflect" is a brand promise. Confirm Retention Manager has the SOP to actually respond within 24 hours, otherwise the promise breaks on contact.
