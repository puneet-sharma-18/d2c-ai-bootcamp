# Instagram Static 04 - The quote on the wall

**Channel**: Instagram Static (single tile, set as a profile pin)
**Calendar slot**: Day 12, May 25 Sun
**Theme served**: Spit-free (VoC theme 2); the wall-quote line from the VoC top-read
**Source**: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md, "the quote that should be on a wall"

---

## Image brief
Plain cream background. The quote in serif type, centered, taking 60% of the tile. Below in smaller type, the attribution. No logo, no product. The quote is the asset.

## Quote on the image (verbatim from VoC)
"I cannot remember the last time I ate a paan I did not have to spit out. This is the first."

## Attribution under the quote
{customer-A}, Almond Delight review, April 2026.

## Caption (under the post)
We have customers writing reviews that read like recovered memories. This one is from April. The customer's name is held back at her request.

We did not invent this idea. The eat-and-swallow paan has been around for 2,000 years. We are putting it back into a box.

Almond Delight, ₹299. Tobacco-free. Hand-roasted. Allergen note: contains tree nuts.

---

**Why this works**: The VoC report named this quote "the one that should be on a wall". This piece puts it there. The single-image format reads as a brand statement, not a sales push. Reposted and screenshotted-forwarded behaviour is the predictable lift.

**CTA option**: "Tap the bio to read the SKU page. Or send this to the friend who has been waiting to try paan again."

## SAFETY FLAGS
- Customer quote requires written consent before paid use. Organic post is fine on anonymised attribution; paid use requires signed release. Retention Manager to confirm.
- Anonymisation: ensure no identifying review metadata is reachable from the customer review on the SKU page if linked.
- Allergen note carries the standard tree-nut disclosure.
