## Caption
Customer reviews call this one "festival in a box".

The base is the same meetha paan we have made for 3 years. Rose, gulkand, fennel, cardamom. The fill is cashew, almond slivers and raisin, layered, not folded in. Each paan has a count: 3 cashew pieces, 4 almond slivers, 6 raisins. Counted.

The reason for the count is that paan with dry fruit is mostly mid-priced category code for "we added cashew dust and called it premium". We did not want to do that.

₹259 for a box of 6. Tobacco-free, hand-roasted, named ingredients.

Allergen note: contains tree nuts (almond, cashew). No dairy. The cashew is a confirmed allergen, not a cross-contact.

Held back from 32-degree pin codes in peak summer. Honest answer.
