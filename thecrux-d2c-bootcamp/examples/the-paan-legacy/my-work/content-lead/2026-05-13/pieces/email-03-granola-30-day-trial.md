# Email 03 - Granola Goodness 30-day trial

**Channel**: Email (campaign send, list cut: Granola Goodness purchasers in last 90 days + first-time site visitors who looked at Granola SKU page twice)
**Calendar slot**: Day 13, May 26 Mon
**Theme served**: Granola crossover (VoC theme 4, 12%)
**Source**: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md theme 4; cohort ramp 38% in 60 days

---

## Subject line options
A. "Eat Granola Goodness for 30 mornings. On day 30, our Meetha Paan is on us."
B. "A 30-day trial that ends with a paan."
C. "Day 30: free Meetha Paan. First 50 only."

Recommended: A. Reads as an offer, but the offer is the brand path, not a discount.

## Preview text
First 50 sign-ups. No catch. We just want to see your day 30.

## Body (~280 words)

Hello,

Here is something we have wanted to try for 6 months.

The data says that 38 out of every 100 people who buy our Granola Goodness end up buying a Meetha Paan within 60 days. The Granola is the breakfast they did not know they wanted. The Meetha Paan is the Sunday-lunch ritual that they slip into after the granola earns their trust.

We are going to make that bridge a little less random.

The 30-day trial works like this. You order one pack of Granola Goodness (₹239). You eat it for breakfast (yogurt, milk, dry, whatever works). On day 30, you reply to this email with one sentence about how it landed. We ship you one Meetha Paan box (₹179), no charge.

First 50 sign-ups. We will close the form when 50 are in.

Two notes before you sign up.

One. Granola Goodness is not gluten-free. The oats are oats. If you are celiac, this is not the right trial for you, and I am sorry we do not have a gluten-free version yet.

Two. The Meetha Paan you receive on day 30 is the same Meetha Paan we sell at ₹179. Not a smaller pack. Not a marketing version. The same box.

Sign-up link below. Or reply TRIAL and I will add you to the list manually.

Puneet
Founder, The Paan Legacy

P.S. If you already buy Granola Goodness monthly, you are part of the data we read. Reply HOOKED and I will send you a Meetha Paan on the house, no trial required.

---

**Why this works**: The trial is an offer-shaped piece that does not feel like a discount, because it is not. It is the brand path made visible. The "P.S. HOOKED" line is the bonus loyalty surprise for the existing Granola repeat-buyer, which converts the highest-LTV segment in one reply.

**CTA option**: One sign-up link, one reply CTA, one P.S. reply CTA. All low-commitment.

## SAFETY FLAGS
- "First 50 sign-ups" must be operationalised. Retention Manager set up a hard cap and a confirmation auto-reply.
- "Granola Goodness is not gluten-free. The oats are oats." This is the right disclosure per VoC theme 6 (celiac question). Confirm exact wording with Ops Manager. If oats are certified gluten-free, change.
- Free Meetha Paan unit economics: 50 boxes at ₹179 cost approximately ₹{cost-figure}. Confirm with founder before launch.
- "HOOKED bonus" P.S.: confirm with Retention Manager that the existing-customer segment is identifiable and bounded.
