# Email 04 - Day 30 Almond Delight gifting re-order nudge

**Channel**: Lifecycle email (triggered, day 30 after last Almond Delight gifting order of 4+ boxes)
**Calendar slot**: Day 15, May 28 Wed (always-on, audience-triggered)
**Theme served**: Gifting repeat (VoC theme 3, 15%); corporate persona
**Source**: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md corporate persona "every Diwali"

---

## Subject line options
A. "30 days since your last gifting order. Keeping you a slot."
B. "The follow-up nobody sent you."
C. "Diwali is in 5 months. Honest count."

Recommended: A. Specific, on-voice.

## Preview text
Not a discount. Just a planning note.

## Body (~230 words)

Hello,

A month ago you ordered Almond Delight in volume. {dynamic-tag: 8 boxes / 24 boxes / 60 boxes / 100+ boxes} to {dynamic-tag: a Mumbai address / a Bangalore address / an HR department / a family event}.

Most of our customers who gift Almond Delight in May come back to us in September-October for the Diwali round. We are writing today, in late May, for one reason: production planning.

Our August-October cashew and gulkand orders go in by July 31. If you are planning a Diwali order in the 50-200 box range, telling us now lets us buy your share of cashews on time. Telling us in October means we will sell out by the 18th, which is what happened to us in 2025.

Three things you can do, in order of commitment.

One. Reply "INTEREST" with a range (10-25 / 25-60 / 60+). No payment. We add you to the production-plan list.

Two. Reply "BROCHURE" and we send the 2-page Diwali brochure with the price grid and custom-sleeve options.

Three. Wait until the public pre-order opens July 30. You will not be at the front of the queue, but you will still get a box.

The Almond Delight you ordered last month is the same SKU that will ship in October. Same slivered-almond recipe. Same rose-petal supplier. Same hand-roast schedule. Tobacco-free, named ingredients, FSSAI registered.

Puneet
Founder, The Paan Legacy

P.S. The custom sleeve takes 3 weeks once we have your logo file. If you are planning a 100+ order, factor the sleeve lead time.

---

**Why this works**: Lifecycle email triggered at day 30 catches the corporate gifting customer in the planning window, not the panic window. The "Karachi Bakery did this in May" insight from Market Analyst is invisible but informs the timing. Three CTAs of escalating commitment lets the customer self-select.

**CTA option**: Three in-body reply CTAs. No promotional button.

## SAFETY FLAGS
- Dynamic tags must resolve correctly per customer record. If a customer is in a tier without a clear order count, default to a neutral "your team's order".
- "Sold out by October 18 in 2025" specific date needs founder confirmation against 2025 records (same flag as email-01).
- "Cashew and gulkand orders go in by July 31" needs Ops Manager confirmation. If a different date, change.
- No regulated claims used.
