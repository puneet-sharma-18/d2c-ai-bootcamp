## Body

Hello,

Four things from May, plain and counted.

**1. Almond Delight is still the bestseller, and we now know why.**

We pulled 94 customer messages from the last 30 days. 17 of them mentioned Almond Delight. 14 of those 17 were gifting-related. People are buying Almond Delight not for themselves but for someone else. That changes how we are pricing the Diwali boxes (more on this below). If you have an Almond Delight story, reply. We read every one.

**2. The Diwali Corporate Gift Box is real and the pre-order opens July 30.**

We sold out of the Almond Delight stock for B2B orders by October 18 last year. Not doing that again. If you are an HR or family-event person and you ordered from us last festival season, you received a separate email last week with the early-access window. If you did not, reply to this email with "Diwali" and a count range and I will add you to the list.

**3. We are slow on quick commerce. Here is why, and what we are doing.**

Anjali Mukhwas is on Blinkit and BigBasket Now. Bombay Sweet Shop is on Zepto. We are not. The reason is not strategic, it is logistical. Our paan needs to hit you within 5 days of packing, and the 10-minute quick-commerce model puts the box in a hot dark room for 18 hours before it scooters to you. We are piloting Zepto Mumbai in Q2 instead of Q3, on a single-SKU basis (Herbal Mukhwas, which is shelf-stable). One city, one SKU, one month. If it works, we expand.

**4. The summer shipping rule is now in writing.**

If your pin code is showing 32 degrees or above between dispatch and delivery, we ship in an insulated box and we move the dispatch to a Monday or Tuesday. No Friday dispatches in May to September outside the metros. We learned this the hard way in 2025; some of you wrote in. Thank you for writing in. The new SOP is at our help-page if you want the full rule.

Five quotes from our customers in May are linked below. Read one. Forward one.

Puneet
