## Body

Hello,

People ask us about every two weeks when we will be on Blinkit and Zepto and BigBasket Now. Here is the honest answer.

We are slow on quick commerce because our paan does not survive the quick-commerce supply chain in its current form.

The 10-minute delivery model works like this. A box sits in a dark room at the local fulfilment hub. Sometimes for 6 hours, sometimes for 36. Then a rider picks it up on a scooter, in a thermal bag, and rides it to your address. In Bombay in May, that is a 32-degree dark room for 18 hours, then a 35-degree scooter ride.

The Almond Delight is fine for 5 days at room temperature in the box we ship from our kitchen. It is not fine for 24 hours in a quick-commerce hub. The gulkand softens. The rose petals lose their oil. The almond goes from a 7-out-of-10 sliver to a 4-out-of-10 chew. The customer who orders us on Blinkit and gets a soft paan blames us, not the supply chain. We do not get to explain.

So we are doing it carefully.

Our pilot is committed for Q3 2026. We are also evaluating whether to bring it forward by one quarter on a single-SKU basis. The candidate is Herbal Mukhwas (shelf-stable for 90 days at room temperature in a sealed pouch), on Zepto, in Mumbai only. One SKU, one platform, one city, one month. When we lock the date, you will hear it from this newsletter first.

If the pilot works, we add a second SKU in a later window. The paan SKUs probably stay on D2C and on aggregators (Zomato and Swiggy, which is restaurant-style not warehouse-style, no dark-room time). We will tell you when we know.

The reason for telling you this in a newsletter is that one of you asked me last week why we were "missing out on the quick-commerce wave". We are not missing out. We are choosing to not ship paan that arrives soft.

Puneet

The Q2 acceleration is named as an evaluation, not a commitment, until the founder locks the decision.
