# Email 02 - Day 7 after your first Almond Delight box

**Channel**: Lifecycle email (triggered, day 7 after first Almond Delight order)
**Calendar slot**: Day 8, May 21 Wed (audience-triggered, always-on)
**Theme served**: Repeat purchase signal; persona-1 ritual buyer
**Source**: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md family-ritual persona

---

## Subject line options (pick one A/B)
A. "How did Sunday land?"
B. "Day 7. The honest question."
C. "Did the in-laws have an opinion?"

Recommended: A. Tight, ritual-aware, not salesy.

## Preview text
Just the one question. Reply if you have a sentence.

## Body (~210 words)

Hello,

A week ago you ordered your first Almond Delight box. By now, if your house works like most of ours, you have had it after a Sunday lunch. Possibly twice.

I have one question. How did Sunday land?

I am asking because the Almond Delight is the SKU we agonise the most over. Slivered almonds, rose petals, gulkand, saffron thread, cardamom, fennel. Each one is a decision. We change suppliers if a batch is even slightly off. So when I ask "how did it land", I am asking if the decisions we make every week actually translated to your Sunday afternoon.

If it landed well, would you write a one-line review on the SKU page? It takes 30 seconds and it is the single biggest thing that helps a stranger in another city decide.

If it did not land, please reply to this email and tell me what was off. The leaf? The sweetness? The almond roast? I read every reply. I will not send you a discount code in response. I will probably ask you a follow-up question.

Either way, thank you for letting us be the post-meal moment in your house this Sunday.

Puneet
Founder, The Paan Legacy

P.S. If your in-laws had an opinion, theirs is the one I really want to hear.

---

**Why this works**: Persona-1 worry is "will my husband / in-laws like it". The P.S. surfaces that exact worry and invites the answer. The "I will not send you a discount code" line is the trust closer. VoC theme of ritual-after-Sunday-lunch directly served. Repeat purchase rate on lifecycle email day-7 is the highest lever for persona-1.

**CTA option**: Two soft, both reply-based. One on-page review link.

## SAFETY FLAGS
- The trigger is "first Almond Delight order, day 7". Retention Manager must confirm the trigger condition; if the customer has ordered other SKUs before, the "your first Almond Delight" framing still works.
- "I read every reply" is a founder promise. Confirm bandwidth or route through a founder-tagged inbox.
- No regulated claims used.
