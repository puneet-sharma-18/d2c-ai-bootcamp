# Email 01 — A calendar reminder, not a subscription

**Channel**: Email (campaign send)
**Calendar slot**: Day 4, May 18 Sun
**Theme served**: Subscription friction (VoC theme 5 partial / repeat-buyer voice)
**Source**: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 5 + repeat-buyer voice + WhatsApp Thread 5

---

## Subject line
A small new thing for our regulars

## Preheader
Not a subscription. Not auto-pay. Just a reminder.

## Body

Hi {first_name},

You asked. Several of you asked. So we built it.

If you have ordered from us more than twice, you can now opt in to a Reminder Service. Here is exactly how it works:

- You tell us roughly how often you finish a box (most regulars say 5 to 6 weeks for the Meetha Paan, 4 to 5 weeks for Almond Delight if it is a family-of-four Sunday ritual).
- About 5 days before that cycle ends, you get a one-line WhatsApp from us: "Time for the next box?" with a one-click reorder link.
- That is it. No auto-charge. No card on file you have to remember to update. No 12-step cancellation flow. Reply STOP and we stop.

We are not doing a subscription yet. The honest reason is that auto-pay subscriptions need a different kind of forecasting on our end (we hand-roast the mukhwas in small batches; we cannot ship Almond Delight in 24 hours if 200 subscriptions fire on the same Monday). The Reminder Service does not break that. It just removes the "ugh I forgot to reorder" friction.

If you want in, reply to this email with "REMIND" and the SKU you want reminded about. We will set it up by hand for the first 100 customers and automate it for the rest later.

If you do not want this, ignore this email. Your Sunday ritual is none of our business until you tell us otherwise.

Best
Puneet

P.S. The Diwali Corporate Gift Box pre-order window opens July 30. If your HR team or family is planning a 50+ box order, reply to this email with "DIWALI" and we will send you the brochure now. Karachi Bakery opened theirs in May; we are opening ours when the products are ready, not before.

---

**Why this works**: Addresses the VoC theme 5 (subscription friction) without overpromising what we cannot yet ship (full subscription). The honesty about "why not full subscription yet" is on-brand. Pre-empts the Karachi Bakery corporate-gifting move in the P.S.

**CTA option**: "REMIND" reply triggers founder DM. "DIWALI" reply triggers brochure send.

## SAFETY FLAGS
- "Karachi Bakery opened theirs in May" is a competitor mention by name. Keep in customer-only email; do NOT repeat in paid creative or on the website.
