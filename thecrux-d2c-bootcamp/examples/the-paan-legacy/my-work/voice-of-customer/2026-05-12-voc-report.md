Date: 2026-05-12
Sample size: 94 messages across 5 sources
Source mix: reviews 38, tickets 22, WhatsApp 18, social 9, CRM 7
Date range of inputs: 2026-04-10 to 2026-05-11

> **Fictional data.** Names anonymised as {customer-A}, {customer-B} etc. Quotes are illustrative.

## Top read in 5 lines
1. Biggest theme is "tastes like my grandmother's paan" (19 of 94 messages, 22%, heavy on D2C reviews and WhatsApp gift recipients). Implication: heritage is the trust-entry. The Content Lead should lead the calendar on grandmother-recipe pieces, not on health benefits.
2. Almond Delight is over-performing for the gifting occasion: 14 of 17 mentions of the SKU are gifting-related ("sent to family", "received as Diwali gift"), all positive. The Almond Delight is the festival-gifting hero; price it tighter and bundle it.
3. Granola Goodness is the curiosity SKU: 11 of 14 reviews say "started here, now buy the Meetha Paan too". It is the persona-3 entry that ramps into persona-1 ritual.
4. The "spit-free, swallow-able" promise needs reinforcement in copy. 8 of 94 (9%) first-time WhatsApp pre-purchase questions ask "is this a regular paan that I spit out or do I eat it?". The category education is not done; PDP needs to lead with this.
5. The quote that should be on a wall: "I cannot remember the last time I ate a paan I did not have to spit out. This is the first." — {customer-A}, D2C review, Almond Delight, 2026-04-22.

## Cohort cuts
**First-time buyer voice**: Entry is almost always the Meetha Paan (₹179) or Granola Goodness (₹239). Trigger is "received as a gift, want to try myself" or "Instagram founder content". Barrier is the price-vs-paan-wala compare. Closer is the spit-free + heritage claim combination.

**Repeat buyer voice**: Repeat buyers move from "did I make the right choice" (first-time anxiety) to "what should I buy for X occasion". They are building rituals (Sunday lunch, monthly family dinner) and gifting occasions. They want the Diwali Corporate Gift Box pre-announced. They ask if there is a subscribe-and-save (there is not).

**Gifting buyer voice**: Distinct. They are not eating the paan; they want the receiver to be impressed. They care about: packaging unboxing experience, the brand story (so the receiver knows what to look up), the FSSAI/tobacco-free claim (so the receiver does not think this is a tobacco gift). The packaging is the marketing.
