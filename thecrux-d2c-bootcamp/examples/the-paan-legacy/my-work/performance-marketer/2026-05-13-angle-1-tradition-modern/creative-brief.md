# Creative Brief — Angle 1: Tradition Meets Modernity
Date: 2026-05-13
For: design team + AI image tool

## The angle in one sentence
The Sunday-paan ritual our founder's grandmother used to make, brought back in a box, without tobacco, without spitting, with named ingredients and the founder's hands in the kitchen.

## Why now (data)
- VoC theme 1: heritage memory — 19 of 94 messages (20%)
- Source: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 1
- Market Analyst signal: Bombay Sweet Shop is winning premium paan at ₹350-420 with agency-grade craft. Our heritage angle is what they cannot copy at scale.

## The 3 must-haves in the visual
1. Hands in the frame — preferably the founder's, in the kitchen, mid-craft (hand-slivering almonds, layering gulkand, wrapping the leaf). The hands are the asset.
2. Warm natural light — the visual register is grandmother's kitchen, not designer studio. Slight imperfection (a spilled fennel seed on the counter) is brand-correct.
3. The phrase "Eat, do not spit." in lower-right of every static, every reel close-frame. Wordmark smaller.

## Tone
- Voice always-list (CLAUDE.md Section 7): "Hand-roasted" / "Small batch" / "Eat, do not spit" / "Tobacco-free" / "Named ingredients"
- Voice never-list: do NOT use "gourmet", "artisanal", "premium" (in copy or visual chrome), "authentic", "elevate", "unlock"
- Reading age: Class 8 in Hindi-English Indian register
- Mood reference: A 1980s Indian family Sunday lunch, captured by a documentary filmmaker, not by a brand photographer.

## What it must NOT show
- A paan-wala shop, a paan-wala counter, a tobacco tin. Anywhere. Ever.
- A spitting bin, a paan-stained wall, or any visual cliché of the paan category.
- Stock "happy Indian family" imagery. Real moments only.
- Agency-Mumbai aesthetic (the Bombay Sweet Shop register). We are warmer, slower, less polished by intent.
- Diabetes-related or weight-loss-adjacent framing. FSSAI claim wall.

## Examples to anchor (not copy)
- Cinnamon Hotels & Resorts (early 2000s) for the warm-craft documentary register
- Aman-style food photography (close, natural light, hand-led) for ingredient close-ups
- Honestbee India early product photography for the casual lifestyle frame

## Deliverables
- 3 static hero ads (1:1 + 4:5 + 9:16) on the Almond Delight SKU
- 1 reel storyboard (15s, founder kitchen POV)
- 1 carousel set (5 cards, one per SKU)
- All images delivered with metadata: brand, SKU, angle name, source citation

## SAFETY FLAGS
- The founder appears on camera. Founder is comfortable with this per CLAUDE.md voice. Confirm willingness for paid usage rights (different from organic).
- "Hand-roasted by the founder" must reflect current operations. If kitchen scaled past founder-only roasting, soften.
- Customer quote permissions on file before any quote-card creative goes paid.
