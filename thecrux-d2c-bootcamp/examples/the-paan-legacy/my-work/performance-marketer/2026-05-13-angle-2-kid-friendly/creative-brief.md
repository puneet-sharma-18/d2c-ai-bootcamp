# Creative Brief — Angle 2: Kid-Friendly + Tobacco-Free
Date: 2026-05-13
For: design team + AI image tool

## The angle in one sentence
The paan you can put on the Sunday family table where the kids, the in-laws, the husband and the corporate Diwali list can all have one without the tobacco, the spitting bin, or the awkward "is this drug-laced" question.

## Why now (data)
- VoC theme 2: spit-free, 12 of 94 (13%); 9 of 94 pre-purchase questions still ask "is this spit-out or eat-and-swallow"
- Source: my-work/voice-of-customer/2026-05-12-voc-report.md → Theme 2
- Market Analyst signal: Karachi Bakery opened Diwali corporate gifting May 5 — 5 months ahead of festival; we need to open our corporate funnel now.

## The 3 must-haves in the visual
1. A family-table setting (Sunday lunch register). No paan-wala, no spitting bin, no corner-stall imagery.
2. The phrase "Tobacco-free. Eat, do not spit." rendered legibly. This is the headline promise; it should not need a second look.
3. For the corporate variant: a stack of branded boxes (with the option for custom-logo sleeves visible) on an office-desk register. Not consumer-facing aesthetic.

## Tone
- Voice always-list: "Tobacco-free" / "Eat, do not spit" / "Kid-friendly (age 6+)" / "Named ingredients" / "FSSAI registered"
- Voice never-list: same as Angle 1 + no "ayurvedic medicine", no "weight-loss", no medical health-claim language.
- Reading age: Class 8 Hindi-English. The corporate buyer reads the same register as the family buyer; do not corporate-speak the corporate ad.
- Mood reference: Quiet domestic comfort for consumer ads; quiet office-professional warmth for corporate ads.

## What it must NOT show
- The word "gourmet" or "artisanal" anywhere.
- A paan-wala, a tobacco tin, a spitting bin (even in negative framing).
- A medical / wellness / weight-loss frame.
- Children's faces (use hands only) or any imagery that could be read as "marketing to kids under 6".

## Examples to anchor (not copy)
- Tata Salt's "Desh ka namak" emotional register for the family-table ads
- A premium B2B office-supply brand register for the corporate Diwali ads (think Bombay Shaving Company's B2B comms tone)

## Deliverables
- 3 consumer family-table statics (1:1 + 4:5 + 9:16)
- 1 corporate-Diwali static + 1 corporate carousel
- 1 reel storyboard (10s, "no spitting bin" anti-positioning)

## SAFETY FLAGS
- Corporate landing page must be live before Ad 4 (carousel) ships to ads. Confirm with Ops.
- "Age 6+" must be the documented FSSAI / brand age recommendation, not a marketing convenience.
- Customer quote (Ad 3 for the social-proof corporate variant) needs paid-usage signed permission.
