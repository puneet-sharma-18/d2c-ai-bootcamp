# Gap analysis — Masala Cranberry Paan

**Current page emphasises**: Generic claims ("gourmet", "artisanal", "made with love"). Three CLAUDE.md banned words used in the bullets ("gourmet", "premium" implied via title, "artisanal"). "Fusion" framing without specifying what the masala blend actually contains.

**What customers actually care about (top theme from VoC)**: From the 11 mentions of Masala Cranberry in VoC, the theme is "Cranberry is a strange ingredient in paan but it works." (theme 3 sentiment for this SKU, +5 net). Customers buy out of curiosity and stay because the flavour clicks. The PDP does not address the curiosity.

**What competitors emphasise that we do not**: Bombay Sweet Shop's new premium paans (₹350-420) explain the modern-flavour rationale on the PDP ("rose-saffron is the chef's interpretation of the classical Lucknowi flavour"). Our Masala Cranberry has no such rationale; it just asserts "fusion".

**What the page does not address that 30%+ of pre-purchase questions ask**: Allergens. Tree nuts are present in 4 of 6 SKUs; Masala Cranberry includes almond. The PDP does not call this out. VoC theme 6 (8 of 94 messages) flagged allergen prominence as a friction.

**The single biggest conversion blocker (best guess)**: The PDP describes the product in agency language ("fusion of traditional and modern") rather than explaining specifically what the masala blend is and why cranberry was the choice. The curiosity-buyer needs the rationale to convert.
