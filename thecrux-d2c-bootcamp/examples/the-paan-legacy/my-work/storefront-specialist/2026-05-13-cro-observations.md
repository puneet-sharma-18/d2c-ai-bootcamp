# CRO Observations — The Paan Legacy Storefront — 2026-05-13

## Observation 1: Allergen disclosure is below the fold on 4 of 6 PDPs
- **Where**: Almond Delight, Masala Cranberry, Dry Fruit Meetha Paan, Granola Goodness PDPs
- **The issue**: Allergens (tree nuts, sesame, oats, dairy in some SKUs) appear in the "specifications" section near the bottom of the page. VoC theme 6 shows 8 of 94 messages flag allergen friction at pre-purchase.
- **Evidence**: VoC support tickets + WhatsApp Thread 2 (the celiac kid case).
- **Suggested fix**: Add an "Allergen disclosure" chip directly under the hero image on every SKU page. Three icons + the named allergens. Hyperlink to the full ingredient list.
- **Impact estimate**: High (compliance + conversion + brand-safety on a regulated category)
- **Effort**: Day

## Observation 2: Banned-word audit (CLAUDE.md voice rules)
- **Where**: All 6 SKU PDPs + the home page
- **The issue**: "Gourmet" appears in 5 of 6 PDPs. "Premium" in 4. "Artisanal" in 3. All three on CLAUDE.md "never" list.
- **Evidence**: Manual scan, 2026-05-13.
- **Suggested fix**: Bulk find-and-replace pass. Replace "gourmet" with "hand-roasted" or "small-batch" depending on context. Drop "premium" entirely. Replace "artisanal" with the specific process ("hand-roasted in small batches" or similar).
- **Impact estimate**: Medium (brand voice consistency, slight conversion lift on family-ritual persona who already filters out marketing-speak)
- **Effort**: Day

## Observation 3: Corporate gifting landing page does not exist
- **Where**: Site-wide — there is no /corporate page
- **The issue**: 18% of annual revenue is corporate gifting (Oct-Nov peak). Karachi Bakery launched their corporate page on May 5. We are blind on this funnel.
- **Evidence**: VoC theme 3 (gifting moment, 15%) + WhatsApp Thread 3 ({customer-G}, 80-box order) + Market Analyst Karachi Bakery signal.
- **Suggested fix**: Build /corporate page with: pricing tiers, custom-logo packaging mockups, FSSAI license, the founder's email for inquiries, a brochure download CTA. The corporate ad in Performance Marketer Angle 2 routes here.
- **Impact estimate**: High (5 months of corporate-funnel buildup before October peak; missing the May-July window is irrecoverable in 2026)
- **Effort**: Week

## Observation 4: Trust signals (FSSAI, tobacco-free) buried
- **Where**: All SKU pages + cart + checkout
- **The issue**: "Tobacco-free" appears on PDPs but not in the cart or checkout. FSSAI license number appears in the specifications block but not as a trust badge near the Add-to-Cart button.
- **Evidence**: VoC theme 2 (spit-free, 13%) is still surfacing as a pre-purchase question; this is a category education failure that PDP trust signals could close.
- **Suggested fix**: Add a "Tobacco-free | FSSAI | Eat, do not spit." trust strip directly above the Add-to-Cart button on every SKU. Repeat the strip on the cart and the checkout success page.
- **Impact estimate**: Medium-High (closes the #2 VoC theme at the conversion moment)
- **Effort**: Day

## Observation 5: Quick-commerce parity check
- **Where**: D2C site vs Zomato + Swiggy listings
- **The issue**: Zomato and Swiggy listings (4.7+ ratings) lead with generic "Gourmet paan box, 6 varieties". The new draft (`my-work/content-lead/marketplace/zomato-01-almond-delight.md`) leads with "Tobacco-free, Eat-don't-spit". The D2C PDP for the same SKU still leads with "A modern twist on a classic Indian flavour". Inconsistent.
- **Evidence**: Cross-channel listing audit, 2026-05-13.
- **Suggested fix**: Align hero copy across D2C, Zomato and Swiggy. Pick one promise ("Tobacco-free, eat-do-not-spit") and apply uniformly. The D2C PDP can carry the longer founder voice underneath.
- **Impact estimate**: Medium (brand consistency lifts trust on multi-channel browsing customers)
- **Effort**: Day
