## Description (~300 words)
Cranberry was not in our first product plan.

The original idea for a "modern paan" SKU was a chocolate paan. We made it. We tasted it. It was bad. Chocolate competes with paan; it does not complete it. We threw it out.

The second idea was tamarind. Closer to authentic but the masala fought back. We threw that out too.

The third idea was cranberry. It worked. The cranberry brings a bright, slightly tart note that the Indian masala blend (fennel, sesame, coriander seed, black salt, black pepper) was missing. Without it the masala was muddy. With it the masala is recognisable, one ingredient at a time.

This is not a fusion paan. It is a masala paan with the brightness the masala needed.

You will not find cranberry in a traditional Lucknowi paan. We did not invent the masala (it is the saunf-mukhwas spice blend, slightly modified). We did add the cranberry. That is the modern part. The traditional part is the leaf, the wrapping, the eat-do-not-spit promise, the tobacco-free guarantee, and the hand-roasting.

A note on the allergens: this SKU contains tree nuts (almond) and sesame. We list every ingredient by name on the back of the box; if you or someone you are gifting to has a known allergy, please read the back before buying.

Best paired with a slightly tannic black tea or a strong coffee. After a south-Indian meal it works as a digestive close. After a north-Indian meal it works as a flavour contrast. After a western meal it works as a curiosity. We have tried all three.

## FAQ
1. **Is it kid-friendly?** Yes, age 6 and above. The masala is mild. The cranberry is tart but not unpleasant for kids.
2. **Why cranberry and not something more traditional?** Because the masala blend needed a bright note and cranberry was the one that worked after chocolate and tamarind failed.
3. **What is the shelf life?** 15 days from the day the box ships. We hand-roast and pack the same week, so the 15-day window starts fresh.
4. **Allergens?** Tree nuts (almond), sesame seeds. Full ingredient list on the back of the box.
5. **Is it tobacco-free?** Yes. Every paan from us is tobacco-free, supari-free, and meant to be swallowed.
