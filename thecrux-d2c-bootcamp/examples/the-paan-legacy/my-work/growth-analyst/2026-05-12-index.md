## Headline

Solid revenue week. The metric to watch sits on the operations side. Summer pin-code complaint rate climbed 3.2 percentage points week on week, concentrated on Almond Delight and Dry Fruit Meetha Paan shipping to Delhi, Hyderabad and Lucknow.

## The one alarm

Summer pin-code complaint rate moved from 2.1% to 5.3%. 7 complaints in 7 days against 3 the prior week. Two complaints are public, one Instagram comment and one Google review at 2 stars. The Meta campaign Summer Gifting Almond Delight Hot Cities (META-004) scaled spend from Rs 2,610/day to Rs 6,235/day in the same window.

## Recommended action

Activate Ops SOP-01 (Summer Shipping) by EOD Tuesday on the Delhi, Hyderabad and Lucknow lanes. Insulated boxes plus gel pack plus courier switch, roughly Rs 4,800 per month. Founder calls the 7 complaint customers Monday and Tuesday. Pause META-004 until SOP-01 is live. Owner: Ops Manager plus founder.
