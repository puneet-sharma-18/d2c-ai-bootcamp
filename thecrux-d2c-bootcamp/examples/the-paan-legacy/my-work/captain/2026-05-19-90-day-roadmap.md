## The brand in one paragraph
The Paan Legacy is a premium gourmet paan and mukhwas brand at ARR ~₹2.8Cr (6 SKUs, 60% D2C, 25% Zomato + Swiggy, 10% franchise outlets, 5% corporate gifting that spikes 8x in Oct-Nov). Founded by Puneet Sharma. The brand thesis is "tradition meets modernity, without the tobacco and without the spitting". Primary persona is the family-ritual buyer; secondary is the corporate Diwali gifting buyer; tertiary is the curious health-modern. The Almond Delight ₹299 is the gifting hero; the Meetha Paan ₹179 is the entry; the Granola Goodness ₹239 is the curiosity ramp.
