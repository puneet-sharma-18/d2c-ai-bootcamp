# Vendor Email — Courier Summer Cold-Chain Escalation
Use when: Two consecutive months of >3% summer-melt complaints from a specific Tier-2 hub.
Tone: professional, direct, founder-grade. Short sentences.

## Subject lines (3 variants)
1. {hub} summer route: SLA breach pattern, need a call
2. Insulated-lane performance review request: {hub}
3. May-July summer SLA: 3-month roadmap needed

## Body

Hi {account-manager-name},

Writing to flag a pattern on the {hub} hub for the second consecutive month.

Customer complaints citing "warm box" / "melted product" / "soft texture" are running at {N}% of orders for the {pin-code-range} range, vs our SLA-implied <2% on cold-chain lanes. Specifically:

- {N} customer complaints in May 2026 alone, vs {N} in April
- {N} returns vs {N} in April
- Sample order IDs: {id-1}, {id-2}, {id-3}

We currently pay the +₹40/box premium for cold-chain on summer-insulated lanes. The product damage rate suggests the gel pack is melting before final delivery on these specific lanes. Two questions:

1. What is the root cause? Hub transit time, final-mile time, gel-pack quality, sortation heat exposure?
2. What is the corrective action plan for the remaining May to September window? Specifically: can {courier-B} guarantee <30h hub-to-doorstep on the {pin-code-range}, or do we need to switch to {alternative-courier} for these lanes?

I would like a call this Thursday or Friday with a 14-day action plan and a hard date for SLA restoration.

Best
Puneet
Founder, The Paan Legacy

## What to attach
- 3 sample order IDs as CSV with date placed, date delivered, transit hours, complaint summary
- Screenshot of one customer's "warm box" message (PII redacted) for credibility

## What NOT to write
- Do not threaten contract termination on the first written escalation. That is a phone call.
- Do not name {alternative-courier} as a real switch threat in writing on the first round — keep it as a "we may have to consider" position to negotiate.

## If they do not reply within 3 days
- Phone call to the account manager. Escalate to their head of operations if needed.
- Trigger SOP-01 "summer pause" variant on the affected lane until resolution.
