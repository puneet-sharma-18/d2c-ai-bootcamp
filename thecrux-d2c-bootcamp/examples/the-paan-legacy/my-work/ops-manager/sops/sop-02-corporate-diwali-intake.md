# SOP — Corporate Diwali Order Intake
Owner: founder, until delegated to a B2B coordinator
Trigger: Inquiry on /corporate page form, OR a WhatsApp message tagged "DIWALI" or "BULK", OR a referral email from an existing corporate customer.
First time vs steady state: First time per customer needs the full 8-step intake; steady-state repeat customers skip steps 2-3.

## When to use this
VoC corporate-gifting persona accounts for ~10% of annual revenue and 18% in Oct-Nov. Karachi Bakery opened their corporate window May 5 2026; we are responding now. Every inquiry needs to be locked by July 30 so production fires on time for October delivery.

## The 8-step playbook

1. **Acknowledge within 4 hours** (auto + personal). Auto-reply with the brochure PDF link. Personal WhatsApp/email follow-up within 4 hours during business days from the founder or B2B coordinator.

2. **Discovery questions** (first time only, 15 min). Box count target. Per-box budget. Delivery window. Custom packaging y/n. Preferred SKU mix (Almond Delight default, +/- Dry Fruit or Meetha for variety). Anaphylactic allergies in the recipient list?

3. **Sample box** (first time only, 5 min ops). Send a 1-box sample at our cost. Tracking number to the buyer within 24 hours.

4. **Quote generation** (per order, 10 min). Use `vendor-kit/corporate-quote-template.md` with the SKU mix, custom packaging delta (+₹40/box if logo sleeve), shipping included. Quote valid 14 days.

5. **Decision point — lock-in deadline**. Buyer must confirm by July 30 for October delivery. Founder decides if late confirmations (Aug 1-15) can be accepted based on production capacity — typical answer: yes with a 10% premium.

6. **PO + advance**. 50% advance on confirmation. Balance 7 days before delivery. PO with packaging design (if custom) attached.

7. **Production schedule** (per order, scheduled). Slot in the production calendar by SKU. Custom packaging artwork to vendor by August 31 latest for October dispatch.

8. **Delivery + post-delivery**. Coordinate delivery date with the buyer. Personal founder WhatsApp on dispatch day. Post-delivery: ask the buyer to forward us 3 receiver responses (with permission) for next year's social proof.

## Decision points
- **Step 5**: late confirmation acceptance (Aug 1-15) is the founder's judgment call based on production capacity.
- **Step 6**: if a corporate customer asks for 0% advance and full payment on delivery, founder decides based on relationship history. Rule of thumb: existing customers and Fortune 500 brands yes; first-time small companies no.

## Escalation
- Order size >100 boxes: founder personally handles every touchpoint.
- Custom packaging requires a 30-day buffer from artwork sign-off to dispatch. If less than 30 days, decline custom packaging on that order.

## Done definition
- Every corporate inquiry acknowledged within 4 hours
- Sample box shipped to first-time inquiries within 48 hours
- PO confirmed by July 30 for October delivery
- 50% advance received before production scheduling
- Custom packaging artwork locked by August 31
