## Top pick, @kitchen.in.kolkata (cre_028)

**Why this creator**
Indrani is a Kolkata-based Bengali food YouTuber with 184k subscribers, a 4.4% ER and 92% credibility. Three reasons she leads the shortlist:

1. **Festive food fluency**: her content cadence spikes in September (Pujo), October-November (Diwali), and January (Pongal). The Paan Legacy's revenue spikes in exactly the same calendar windows (CLAUDE.md Section 2 flags Oct-Nov skews corporate gifting to 18% of revenue).
2. **NRI diaspora tail**: 26% of her audience is US + UK + Canada + Australia. The Paan Legacy ships internationally via D2C for Diwali gifting; this is the cleanest fit-to-NRI persona in the pool.
3. **Bengali audience overlap**: paan culture is strongest in Bengal and Maharashtra. Indrani's 22% Kolkata + Bengali NRI base maps to a paan-eating audience the brand under-indexes on today (CLAUDE.md Section 7 names franchise outlets only in Mumbai, Pune and Delhi).

**The fit on the brand DNA**
- Audience: 81% female, 35-44 age weight (41%), 74% India + 26% Western diaspora. Maps to Section 5 primary persona ("family ritual buyer, 32 to 48").
- Voice: warm, heritage-rooted, modern. The exact three CLAUDE.md voice words. Her recipe essays read like the brand's own founder copy.
- Price tier: her audience pays for Tata Sampann (premium pulses) and Fortune oil (premium edible oil) per `recent_paid_collabs`. The Paan Legacy's ₹179-₹299 SKU band sits comfortably above commodity paan and below luxury sweets, which is her audience's price comfort.
- Format: YouTube long-form (5-12 min) suits the heritage story better than IG reels. The "how to end a Bengali meal" video format is the natural integration.

**The five red-flag checks**
1. Credibility: PASS (8% fake-follower share)
2. Audience geo: PASS (74% India, the diaspora tail is shippable)
3. Engagement for tier: PASS (4.4% on a 184k mid YT creator is healthy)
4. Saturation: PASS (18% paid-post ratio)
5. Competitor traps: PASS. No direct paan competitor in her 90-day list. Tata Sampann is adjacent food, not paan.

Decision: SHORTLIST (top priority for Diwali season; book by August latest)

**Rate anchor**
- Platform estimate: ₹1,80,000-2,80,000 for a dedicated 8-min cooking video, or ₹80,000-1,30,000 for a 60-90s integration inside an existing video
- Anchor counter for the integration: ₹56,000
- Walk price for the integration: ₹48,000

We anchor on the integration (not the dedicated video) because the integration buys season-relevance and audience trust at one-third the rate, and is renewable if it performs.

## Walk recommendation, @wellness.with.r (cre_008)

**Why she looks like the obvious buy**
2.1M followers, "wellness" tag, the immediate name a founder would suggest if asked "who should we partner with for Diwali wellness". Mega-tier reach.

**Why we walk**
- 41% fake-follower share. Of the 2.1M, ~860k are bots or dormant.
- 49% of audience is outside India (Bangladesh + Pakistan + Indonesia + UAE dominant). The Paan Legacy ships to India and select diaspora markets; ~40% of her audience cannot order.
- 64% paid-post ratio in last 30d. Audience is sponsorship-fatigued, paid-post ER drops to 0.6-0.8%.
- Agency-gated (no direct email). Rate negotiation runs through an agency that pads 25-40% on top of the creator's quote.

Same budget (one Rhea reel = ~₹8L) buys: Indrani integration + Karan thought piece + Meera reel + Devika integration + Pia reel + the entire 4-week ladder, with budget left for amplification. Strictly better return.

## Why the shortlist is what it is (one-line read for the founder)

The Paan Legacy does not have a paan-creator pool. The shortlist instead buys three different angles in parallel: (1) Indrani for festive-food season and Bengali-NRI gifting, (2) Karan for B2B corporate Diwali bulk orders, (3) Meera + Devika + Pia for the ritual-and-wellness audience that converts on the Meetha Paan and Almond Delight SKUs. Total commit at anchor counter ~₹2.25L across 5 posts in the September-October window.

If you only have budget for one, start with Karan. Corporate Diwali B2B has the highest order-size-to-creator-cost ratio in this shortlist (one HR-team buying 50 Diwali Gift Boxes covers the cost of his post 6x over). Indrani is the close second pick if the brand wants D2C festive-season conversion over B2B.
