# Email Template — Welcome / First Purchase Follow-up
Trigger: 7 days after first-ever order
Segment: First-time buyers, all SKUs
Tone: Founder voice, warm, signed by Puneet personally
Allowed personalisation tokens: {first_name}, {first_sku_name}, {customer_city_if_known}

## Subject line (3 variants)
1. About the paan you just opened
2. {first_name}, a small note from Puneet
3. Why your {first_sku_name} probably looks different

## Body
Hi {first_name},

You bought your first box from us a week ago. Puneet here, founder. I write this email by hand to every first-time customer for our first ~6 months. After that I will probably have to stop, so I am taking advantage while I still can.

A few things you might notice about what is in the box:

The paan is wrapped to be eaten whole. Not chewed-and-spit. We left out tobacco and supari on purpose. The whole point of our brand is that paan was a digestive ritual long before it became a tobacco vehicle. The "eat, do not spit" promise is on the box for a reason.

The back of the box has every ingredient named. No "natural blend" or "ayurvedic extract" placeholders. If you are allergic to anything in the box, the back label is honest about it.

If something is not right — texture off, packaging damaged, the experience did not match the expectation — hit reply on this email. Not the support address. I will personally see it.

Two other things you might find useful:

If you finish the box and want a reminder for the next one (without a subscription), reply REMIND and I will set you up on our Reminder Service.

If you have a Diwali corporate gifting list at your office or in your family, our window opens July 30 (yes, that early — Karachi Bakery is already running theirs). Reply DIWALI and I will send the brochure.

Welcome to The Paan Legacy.

Puneet
Founder

P.S. If you want to know what is actually in the {first_sku_name} — like, the ingredient-by-ingredient walk-through — that is at thepaanlegacy.com/ingredients/{first_sku_handle}. Reading the back of a paan box is rarely useful at most brands. With us, it is the whole point.

## CTA
Soft. Replies trigger personal founder response (REMIND, DIWALI, or anything else).

## When NOT to send
- Customer has an open support ticket
- Customer's order was returned / refunded
- Email bounced on order confirmation

## Compliance
- Promotional category (mentions Diwali pre-order, Reminder Service). Footer: unsubscribe link mandatory.
