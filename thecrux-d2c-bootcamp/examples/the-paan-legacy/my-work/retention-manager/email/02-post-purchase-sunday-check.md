# Email Template — Post-Purchase Sunday Lunch Check (Almond Delight / Meetha Paan)
Trigger: 7 days after an Almond Delight or Meetha Paan order, no support ticket
Segment: Family-ritual persona, first-time buyer
Tone: Founder voice, low-pressure, anchored to the Sunday-lunch ritual
Allowed personalisation tokens: {first_name}, {first_sku_name}

## Subject line (3 variants)
1. Did your Sunday lunch get a paan?
2. {first_name}, one week in
3. The Sunday lunch test

## Body
Hi {first_name},

It is a week since you got your {first_sku_name}. By now you have either:

(a) Eaten it solo over a few evenings,
(b) Saved it for Sunday lunch with the family, or
(c) Set it aside and forgot.

If (a) or (c), we get it. The Sunday-lunch frame is what most of our customers eventually settle into, but it is not the only way.

If (b), we want to know how the family responded. Send me a one-line reply. Our customer feedback shapes the next SKU, the next batch, the next packaging change.

If something went wrong — paan didn't travel well, taste was off, you wanted me to know — hit reply. Same address. I read every one.

Best
Puneet

P.S. If you want one for next Sunday, the Reminder Service can ping you at the 6-week mark. Reply REMIND.

## CTA
- Soft. Reply with anything → personal response.
- "REMIND" reply → enrol in Reminder Service.

## When NOT to send
- Customer has an open complaint or returns ticket
- Customer ordered Almond Delight as a gift (different post-purchase email — gift recipient is the audience, not the buyer)

## Compliance
- Service category (post-purchase outcome check). Unsubscribe link in footer.
