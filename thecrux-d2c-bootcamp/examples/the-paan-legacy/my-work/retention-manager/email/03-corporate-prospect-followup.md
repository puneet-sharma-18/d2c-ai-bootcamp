# Email Template — Corporate Prospect Follow-up (after sample box delivered)
Trigger: 14 days after corporate sample box delivery, no PO yet
Segment: Corporate gifting buyer persona
Tone: Direct, professional, founder-signed. No discount pressure.
Allowed personalisation tokens: {first_name}, {company_name}, {box_count_estimate}

## Subject line (3 variants)
1. {company_name} Diwali planning — quick check
2. {first_name}, did the sample land okay?
3. 60 days to PO lock — where are we?

## Body
Hi {first_name},

Sent your sample box about two weeks ago. Hoping it landed in good shape and that you had a chance to walk it through with your team.

A few things to flag:

The Diwali PO lock-in deadline is July 30. We do this because the production schedule for October delivery has a hard upstream constraint (custom packaging artwork to vendor by August 31; ingredient procurement 6 weeks before pack). Late confirmations between August 1-15 are possible with a 10% premium, but it gets harder after that.

If your team needs more time to decide, that is fine. The best signal I can give: Karachi Bakery's corporate Diwali book is filling up since May 5. The category is moving 4-5 months earlier than 2024.

A direct question: do you want a 15-minute call this week to walk through SKU mix, custom packaging options, or pricing tiers? Reply with two time-slots that work; I will pick one.

If the timing doesn't work for 2026 but you'd like to plan for 2027 in a relaxed way, reply with "2027" and I'll add you to the August-followup track.

Best
Puneet
Founder

P.S. The brochure is attached again in case it got lost in the inbox. If your finance team needs an invoice format or a vendor onboarding doc, those are also attached.

## CTA
Time-slot reply → founder calendars 15-min call.
"2027" reply → moves to August follow-up.
Silence → another email 30 days later, then drop from this year's track.

## When NOT to send
- PO already received
- Buyer has explicitly said "next year"
- Order size too small to be worth the founder's time (< 15 boxes) — handed to a different track

## Compliance
- B2B marketing (covered by inquiry-form opt-in); unsubscribe link mandatory
