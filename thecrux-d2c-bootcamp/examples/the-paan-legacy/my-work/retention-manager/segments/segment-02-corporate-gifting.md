# Segment — Corporate Gifting Buyers

## Definition (rule)
Customers who have placed at least one bulk order (>10 boxes in a single PO), AND order was tagged "B2B" or "Diwali" in CRM, AND email domain is a corporate domain (not gmail/yahoo).

## Size estimate
~85 active corporate buyers (Shopify + CRM cross-reference on 2026-05-13). Concentrated in HR functions of Mumbai/Bangalore/Pune tech and pharma companies.

## What this segment cares about
From VoC corporate-gifting persona:
- Tobacco-free guarantee (non-negotiable for corporate compliance)
- Custom packaging with company logo / message
- Lock-in pricing for advance planning
- Hard delivery dates (Diwali timing)
- An invoice format their finance team accepts
- A founder/founder-equivalent point of contact (not a generic salesperson)

## Templates that fire for this segment
- `whatsapp/03-corporate-diwali-followup.md` — 7 days after inquiry
- `email/03-corporate-prospect-followup.md` — 14 days after sample box delivered
- Annual "save the date" email (sent late April every year to last year's buyers)
- Post-Diwali debrief email (sent November 5, asking for next-year intent)

## How to retarget if needed
- LinkedIn Custom Audience: by job title (HR, Office Manager, Admin, Procurement) at companies in the target segment. Use for the corporate ad creative (Performance Marketer Angle 2 Ad 4).
- Meta Lookalike: build 1% LAL on the corporate buyer list for cold acquisition of new HR contacts.
