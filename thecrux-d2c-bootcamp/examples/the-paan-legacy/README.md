# The Paan Legacy — Second Worked Example

A parallel run-through of the entire D2C workshop on a real-but-illustrative brand: **The Paan Legacy** (thepaanlegacy.com), a premium D2C gourmet paan and mukhwas brand by Puneet Sharma. Same shape as `examples/little-lab/`, different category.

**Why a second example.** Little Lab is baby skincare — paediatrician-trust, CDSCO compliance, ingredient transparency wedge. The Paan Legacy is food — FSSAI compliance, quick-commerce-first distribution (Zomato/Swiggy), heritage-meets-modernity positioning, gifting and corporate B2B channels. The two together show the cohort that the 10-teammate framework holds across categories.

> Brand basics (name, founder, taglines, SKU names) reflect the real site. **All numbers, customer messages, competitor pricing and operational details are fictional**, sized to feel real for the workshop. Do not cite outside the cohort.

## Why this contrast with Little Lab matters in the room

| Dimension | Little Lab | The Paan Legacy |
|---|---|---|
| Category | Cosmetic (baby skincare) | Edible (gourmet paan / mukhwas) |
| Regulator | CDSCO | FSSAI |
| Hero channel | D2C site + Amazon | D2C site + Zomato + Swiggy |
| Trust signal | Paediatric review panel | Heritage + FSSAI + tobacco-free |
| Anti-positioning | "We never say 'natural' or 'toxin-free'" | "We never sell tobacco-laced paan or spit-paans" |
| Distinct revenue line | Marketplace (Amazon/Flipkart) | Quick commerce + franchise + corporate gifting |
| Primary persona | Tier-1 metro tech-aware mom | Tier-1 / Tier-2 family + corporate gifting buyer |
| Cradle-cap moment | Cradle Cap Balm 50ml | Meetha Paan (₹179, entry SKU) |

When an instructor switches from Little Lab to The Paan Legacy halfway through Module 4, the cohort sees the framework is category-agnostic. Same primitives. Different brand. Same outcome.
